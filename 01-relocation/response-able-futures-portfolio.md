## B. The six pages

### Structural decision

**No intro page and no CV page.** Six pages for three projects is already tight, and the CV
and motivation letter carry the career. One thin band at the top of page 1 positions you.
**Two pages per project:** page one is challenge and thinking, page two is process,
decisions and result. Process is a stated requirement, so it gets half the space.

**Format.** Landscape 16:9 (1920 × 1080 px), unless the official page specifies otherwise.
Export as RGB, images at about 150 dpi equivalent, check the file is under 15 MB. Keep the
grid identical across all six pages: the committee reads consistency as care.

**Copy conventions.** Anything in `[square brackets]` is a fact I do not have. **Fill or delete
it. Never leave a guess in.** Each page carries a small "My role" line, because the programme
asks for your contribution in group work explicitly.

**RTL flag.** Pages 1, 2 and 3 will show Arabic. Set Arabic samples in their real direction,
right-aligned, never mirrored or reversed by an export. Check the PDF in a second viewer
before sending, since some PDF exports break Arabic shaping.

### Page 1: The curriculum, the problem

**Key message:** A national curriculum is one design decision repeated a hundred times. Get
the system wrong and every child pays for it.

**Positioning band (top strip, small, the only place your career appears):**

> Sara Radwan. Designer and creative director, Cairo, Doha, Dubai and Abu Dhabi since 2003.
> Most of my work has been for public institutions: schools, ministries, a national
> newspaper. Information that has to be understood by people who did not ask for it.

**Headline:** One system, a hundred books

**Body:**

> In 2019 I was asked to build the visual and typographic system for the UAE's national
> school curriculum. More than 100 books, five subjects, three scripts, and a team of
> illustrators, designers and layout specialists who would apply it.
>
> The hard part was not any single page. It was that a child who meets a rule in one book
> has to meet the same rule in the next, in another subject, sometimes in another script.
> [CONFIRM: grade range and the three scripts.] Consistency is what lets a young reader stop
> decoding the page and start learning from it.
>
> That is the problem I want to work on in health. A medication leaflet and a Grade 3 science
> book fail in the same way.

**My role:** Creative director and team lead. I designed the system and directed the team
that applied it. [CONFIRM: team size.]

**Visuals:** One hero spread from the books, large. A strip of four or five covers across
subjects, small. A short Arabic and English line from the same page side by side.

**Layout:** Band across the top. Headline and body in the left third. Hero spread on the
right two-thirds, bleeding off the edge. Cover strip along the bottom.

**What it shows the committee:** Public-scale systems thinking, design for comprehension,
and a direct line to health literacy, stated in your words rather than claimed.

### The weaknesses, in order of damage

**1. Everything was designed *for* people, under an institutional brief. Nothing was designed
*with* them.** That is the centre of this programme, and the committee will see it. Do not
hide it. Make it the reason you are applying. A suggested spine for the motivation letter,
for you to confirm or reject because it has to be true: *for 22 years I have designed
information for people I rarely met. I want to learn to design it with them.* If that is
true, it turns the biggest gap into the clearest motivation in the pile.

**2. No health or care work.** Allowed by the rules, but most strong applicants will have
something. The *Mother, Baby & Child* swap and any real UNICEF work are the only portfolio
fixes. Otherwise, **put serious design effort into the poster.** It is the one piece made fresh
for this panel, on their subject, and it is your best chance to show care thinking as work
rather than as a claim.

**3. No verified outcomes.** +38% belongs to a project not in the main three. Do not invent
numbers to fill the gap. One honest observation per project is worth more, and a panel of
design researchers will probe numbers in interview.

**4. Seniority can read as "done learning".** The self-critique lines on pages 2 and 6 are
there to answer that. If the copy sounds like a director presenting finished work, it fails
the "accepts critical feedback" test, however good the work is.

**5. Staleness.** The format facts came from an old cached page. A changed page limit or a
new required page would break this plan. Confirm before layout.
