# Weekly homework — Route B, funded study

Owned by the **scholarships session**. Route A homework, if it gets written, belongs in a
separate file so the two sessions never collide.

Built from `pipeline-scholarships.md` and `timeline-to-2027.md`, 21 August 2026.

## How this works

Follows the Monday rhythm in `README.md`: **one concrete action per week, not a backlog.**
Everything else in a week is small — a check, an email, a file saved. If a week slips, the
headline action carries forward and the small items get dropped, not the other way round.

Each week is split:

- **[SARA]** — needs her: her documents, her signature, her professional judgement, her name on an email.
- **[CLAUDE]** — research and drafting I do without her. She reviews the output.

**Granularity is deliberate.** Weekly through early October, because those dates are real.
**Monthly from November**, because week-level precision eight months out is invented. Each
block converts to weeks as it comes into range.

**Time budget: 1–3 hours a week** to end of October, rising sharply in December and January.
She has a demanding full-time job; a plan that assumes otherwise fails in week three.

## The money constraint shapes this whole plan

**She does not have savings now.** The ~€6,000 is a projection — end-of-service gratuity
plus selling possessions — and it only exists **just before travelling**, i.e. *after* the
spring 2027 permit application that would need to show it. So it is **arrival money, not
permit money, and not preparation money.**

**Preparation costs ~€1,200–2,400 and falls Aug 2026 – Aug 2027.** That has to come out of
salary at roughly **€100–200/month**, starting August. Manageable if it starts now;
a problem if it is discovered in December.

**No route requiring proven funds is open**: Austria wants €15,701, Germany ~€13,100.

**Consequence for this plan: only a scholarship that pays a monthly stipend is a real
route.** That is **SISGP (Sweden)** and **Erasmus Mundus**, with Ireland a distant third and
only for a one-year master's. Austria's cheap tuition is a *destination* worth targeting
through Erasmus Mundus — not a way in on its own.

The homework below is weighted accordingly: Sweden and Erasmus Mundus get the effort.

## ⛔ The two things that gate everything else

Neither is a deadline anyone set, which is exactly why they slip.

1. **Egyptian document legalisation — 6–10 weeks, three authorities, run remotely from Abu
   Dhabi.** Austria requires it complete *at the moment of application*. Nothing else in
   this file can be submitted to Austria until it is done.
2. ⛔ **No English language test exists.** *(Corrected 21 Aug — earlier passes wrongly
   recorded one as held. She has never sat IELTS or TOEFL; what she holds is her degree
   certificate.)* Sweden requires **English 6** — IELTS Academic 6.5, no band below 5.5.
   **Book now:** slots fill weeks ahead, and the mid-January admission deadline is fixed.
   The IELTS fee (~€310–350) is the largest single line in the preparation budget.

Both are addressed in weeks 1–3 because everything downstream depends on them.

## Monthly — October 2026 onward

Converts to weekly detail as each block comes into range.

### October 2026 — Erasmus Mundus opens
- Consortium deadlines land through the month and are **staggered, not shared**. Track each
  separately; there is no single date.
- **Submit early.** Some consortia assess on a rolling basis and funded places go first.
- Legalised documents should be back or close. **If they are not, escalate — this is the
  month the Austrian route is won or lost.**
- WU round 1 (8 Oct) passes. Expected; not a failure.

### November 2026 — the Swedish shortlist
- **Mid-Nov: SI publishes the eligible-programme list.** Diary it. Until it exists, no one
  knows whether a design, brand or marketing master's is fundable in Sweden — the single
  biggest open question on the strongest lead.
- Shortlist Swedish programmes and check each one's own entry requirements.
- universityadmissions.se opens for the autumn 2027 intake.

### December 2026 — finish everything
- **All application assets complete by 31 December.** CV, case studies, motivation letters,
  referees briefed, documents legalised and translated.
- Swedish application drafted and ready to submit, not started.
- WU Vienna round 2 application drafted.
- **CIM interaction:** if a Level 7 module or assignment lands this month, it collides
  head-on with the application window. **Check module dates now**
  (`02-cim-level-7/module-tracker.md`) and, if they clash, decide which gives — before
  December rather than during it.

### January 2027 — submit
- **8 Jan: WU Vienna round 2.**
- **Mid-Jan: universityadmissions.se closes.** The gate on everything Swedish.
- Remaining Erasmus Mundus deadlines.
- Nothing new starts this month. Preparation is over.

### February 2027 — the SI scholarship
- **Portal open roughly 9–25 Feb, two weeks only.** Requires an existing Swedish admission.
- The application is where the 22 years finally pays: documented work experience and
  demonstrated leadership are the *qualifying* criteria, not obstacles to explain away.

### March–June 2027 — decisions and permits
- Offers and scholarship results.
- **Residence permit processing: budget one to four months.** Routinely underestimated and
  the step that slips departures.
- Austria: proof of funds ≈ **€15,700** for twelve months. That number needs to exist in an
  accessible account, not in theory.
- Notice at WeDo. **Only at this stage does the confidentiality rule relax, and only once
  something is signed.**
