# LinkedIn search kit

**Built 3 August 2026**, from everything the search learned in its first week. Copy-pasteable
strings, set up once as alerts, then maintained rather than rebuilt.

---

## ⭐ Better than LinkedIn: go straight to company ATS boards

**Added 3 Aug.** The same role gets wildly different competition depending on where it is seen.

| Where | Typical applicant count seen this week |
|---|---|
| LinkedIn, **promoted by hirer** | Berlitz **437** · PwC **102** · ICF Next **49** |
| LinkedIn, not promoted | Breuninger **22** · BENCHMARKED **11** |
| **Company's own ATS board** | **Usually a fraction of either.** Nobody is scrolling these |

**Bookmark the boards, not the search.** Greenhouse, Ashby, Personio, SmartRecruiters and Workday
pages are public, updated the moment a role opens, and carry no promotion budget pushing them
into a hundred thousand feeds.

| Company | Board |
|---|---|
| Black Forest Labs | `job-boards.greenhouse.io/blackforestlabs` |
| Parloa | `job-boards.eu.greenhouse.io/parloagermany` |
| Aleph Alpha | `jobs.ashbyhq.com/AlephAlpha` |
| Breuninger | `jobs.smartrecruiters.com/EBreuningerGmbHCo` |
| Berlitz | `berlitz.careers` |
| gestalten | `work@gestalten.com` — email, no board |
| De Gruyter Brill | `degruyterbrill.com/publishing/about-us/careers/job-openings` |
| Malmberg | `vacatures@malmberg.nl` — open applications invited |

**Check ten boards once a week.** It takes twenty minutes and it puts her in front of roles
before the promotion budget starts.

## The single most important thing: speed, not coverage

**BENCHMARKED had 11 applicants at 10 hours old. PVH had 353.**

That is the whole game. Her craft is competitive; her position in a 353-deep pile is not.
**Being early is the one advantage she fully controls**, and it costs nothing.

So: **narrow searches saved as daily alerts**, not one broad search browsed weekly. Six precise
alerts firing every morning beats an hour of scrolling on a Sunday.

## Search 1 — 🇳🇱 Netherlands. **The sponsorship self-filter.** Highest precision in the kit.

**Location: Netherlands · Date posted: Past week**

```
("Art Director" OR "Brand Designer" OR "Senior Designer" OR "Editorial Designer") AND ("30% ruling" OR "highly skilled migrant" OR "kennismigrant" OR "erkend referent")
```

**Why this one is different from every other search.** Dutch HSM permits require the employer
to be an **IND-recognised sponsor**, and recognised sponsors advertise it — they mention the
30% ruling as a perk, or name the kennismigrant scheme. **Employers who cannot sponsor never
use these words.** So the string filters for legal eligibility before she reads a single
requirement.

**This is the search that would have prevented the egg mistake**, applied to the country where
sponsorship is a hard legal gate rather than a willingness question.

## Search 2 — 🇩🇪 Germany. Primary market, sponsorship-signalled.

**Location: Germany · Date posted: Past 24 hours**

```
("Art Director" OR "Senior Art Director" OR "Brand Designer" OR "Editorial Designer" OR "Senior Designer") AND ("visa" OR "Blue Card" OR "Blaue Karte" OR "relocation" OR "sponsorship" OR "work permit")
```

Germany needs no sponsor register, so this filters for **willingness**, not eligibility. Fewer
hits than the Dutch string, but every hit is an employer who has already thought about it.

---

## Search 3 — 🇩🇪 Germany. English-language ads, which is the real proxy.

**Location: Berlin · Hamburg · Munich · Cologne · Date posted: Past 24 hours**

```
("Art Director" OR "Brand Designer" OR "Senior Designer") AND ("English" OR "international team" OR "English-speaking")
```

**An ad written in English in Germany is usually an English-working office.** That is the
cheapest available signal, and it is the one BENCHMARKED failed — its ad was in German and it
asked for *sichere Kommunikation auf Deutsch*.

---

## Search 4 — 🇮🇪 Ireland. **The most under-worked country relative to how achievable it is.**

**Location: Ireland · Date posted: Past week · no keyword filter beyond the titles**

```
"Art Director" OR "Senior Designer" OR "Brand Designer" OR "Graphic Designer" OR "Design Lead"
```

**Deliberately broad.** Ireland is small enough to read everything, English by default, and the
permit floor is **€36,605** — roughly half the Dutch threshold.

⭐ **And Irish postings frequently carry an explicit sponsorship field** — e.g. *"Available for
Work Visa Sponsorship: No"*. **Read that field first and the job description second.** It is
the only country in the set where the question is answered before applying.

## Search 5 — 📚 Publishing and editorial. EU-wide. **Her rarest credential.**

**Locations: Germany · Netherlands · Belgium · Ireland · Austria · Spain**

```
"Editorial Designer" OR "Publication Designer" OR "Book Designer" OR "Typographer" OR "Layout Designer" OR "Art Director" AND (publishing OR editorial OR magazine OR books)
```

15 of her 22 years. Al Arab, CPI, the national curriculum, the MFA annual report. **Low volume,
so run it as an alert rather than a search** — these roles appear rarely and go fast.

Named employers worth following directly: **gestalten · TASCHEN · Hatje Cantz · Prestel ·
De Gruyter Brill (Leiden and Vienna) · Peeters (Leuven) · Condé Nast Germany · Gruner + Jahr ·
Zeit Verlag**.

## Search 6 — 👗 Fashion and lifestyle art direction. **The BENCHMARKED shape, in English.**

**Locations: Germany · Netherlands · Ireland · Spain · Belgium**

```
"Art Director" AND (fashion OR lifestyle OR beauty OR retail OR "e-commerce")
```

BENCHMARKED proved the profile fits: fashion or lifestyle art direction, **InDesign-led with
Figma only a plus**, craft-over-credential, three years asked where she has twenty-two. **It
failed only on language.** Find the same role in an English-working office.

---

## Search 7 — 🤖 AI-forward creative. Her scarcest and least-used card.

```
("Art Director" OR "Creative Director" OR "Brand Designer" OR "Design Lead") AND ("AI" OR "generative" OR "Midjourney" OR "AI-native")
```

**AI usage guidelines written into client brand systems is governance, not tooling** — and
governance is much rarer than prompt skill. Moss and BENCHMARKED both led on AI. This category
is growing fast and she is genuinely early to it.

## Exclusions — the noise that has wasted the most time

LinkedIn's `NOT` operator is unreliable in job search, so **screen these out by eye rather than
by string.** Every one has already cost real hours:

| Kill on sight | Why |
|---|---|
| **UX Designer · UI Designer · Product Designer** | Different discipline. Cost time on Monks, Elsevier, Wolters Kluwer, RELX |
| **"screens, flows, states, prototypes"** | Product design wearing a creative title |
| **CGI · VFX · 3D · rigging · compositing** | The AKQA ACD trap |
| **Motion Designer · After Effects as a core duty** | She directs motion, she does not build it |
| **"Expertise in Figma, Auto Layout, components"** | Genuine stretch until the 30 Aug artefact exists |
| **Performance marketing · paid social · variant testing as the core** | Not her discipline |
| **Denmark** | Highest permit floor in the set, and it punishes taking a less senior title |

## Titles to search, and titles to skip

**Search these.** Senior IC craft roles, where the portfolio answers the question and no local
network is needed on day one:

> Art Director · Senior Art Director · Editorial Designer · Brand Designer · Senior Brand
> Designer · Senior Designer · Design Lead · Publication Designer · Book Designer · Layout
> Designer · Graphic Designer *(Ireland and Germany only, where the floor allows it)*

**Treat as occasional stretches, not the core of the pipeline.** Every leadership title that
reached a decision was rejected — Creative Director, Head of Creative, Design Director, Creative
Lead. At that level employers buy local market knowledge and an existing network, which
twenty-two years in MENA does not transfer:

> Creative Director · Head of Creative · Design Director · Creative Lead · Head of Brand

## ⛔ Confidentiality — non-negotiable, and LinkedIn is where it bites

**Her employer monitors her LinkedIn activity.** These are the settings and behaviours that
matter:

| Safe — private to her | ⛔ Visible, or has leaked before |
|---|---|
| **Job alerts** | **`#OpenToWork`** — never, in any form, including the recruiters-only setting. It has leaked historically and her boss actively watches |
| **Saved jobs** | **Following EU agencies in bulk** — following can surface in activity. Add them slowly, mixed with normal industry accounts |
| **Applying, including Easy Apply** | **Clicking "I'm interested"** on a company page — this is visible. Already flagged for mobile.de |
| **Recruiter InMail conversations** | **A location field naming an EU city** |
| | **Sudden connection sprees** with EU recruiters |

**Nothing in the search itself is visible. The risk is entirely in the signals around it.**

## Cadence

| When | What |
|---|---|
| **Daily, 10 minutes** | Read the alert emails from searches 2, 3 and 6. Apply same-day to anything under 24 hours old with fewer than ~30 applicants |
| **Weekly** | Searches 1, 4, 5 and 7. Lower volume, higher value |
| **Monthly** | Re-read this file against what has actually converted, and cut the searches that produce nothing |

**The applicant count on the posting is the most useful number LinkedIn shows.** Under 20 means
a real chance. Over 200 means the craft no longer decides it.
