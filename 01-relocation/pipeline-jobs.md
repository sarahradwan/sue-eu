## The rule that matters most

**Never mark sponsorship confirmed unless the source says so explicitly.**

Not sponsorship: "international team", "applicants worldwide", "relocation support"
(may mean flights, not a visa).

Is sponsorship: "we sponsor work visas", named permit route, or the employer appearing on
a national register of licensed sponsors.

Unstated → record as **unverified**, note what is missing. Never present as qualifying.

## Highest-leverage research

Permit routes that do **not** need an employer sponsor — job-seeker visas, orientation-year
permits, talent and self-employment routes. These change what she can even apply to, and
matter more than any individual job posting. Findings go in `country-notes.md`.
