# Timeline — target: EU by mid-2027

Set 25 July 2026. Recalculate whenever a real deadline lands in the pipeline.

## The finding that matters

"Mid-2027" sounds distant. It is not. Working backwards, **the decisive window opens in
roughly three months and closes early next year.**

For a September 2027 intake — the natural target for a 2027 departure — programme
applications generally **open October–November 2026**, with **deadlines mostly falling
November 2026 to January 2027**, some running into February or March.
([Erasmus Mundus guidance](https://erasmus-plus.ec.europa.eu/node/5758))

So the sequence is: **prepare Aug–Sept 2026 → apply Oct 2026–Feb 2027 → decisions and visa
spring 2027 → depart Aug–Sept 2027.**

**Missing that window costs a full year.** The next equivalent intake is September 2028,
which breaks the 2027 target completely. This is the single most important date fact in
the plan.

## Honest note on "mid-2027"

| Route | Realistic departure | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Route B** — funded study | **Aug–Sept 2027** | EU academic year starts September. Slightly later than mid-year, and the closest fit. |
| **Route A** — job + sponsorship | **Genuinely mid-2027** | Roles appear year-round; timing is driven by search + notice + permit processing, not an academic calendar. |

Route A can hit mid-2027. Route B lands early autumn. Both need work starting now, and
running them in parallel is the right call — they have different failure modes and
different calendars, so one does not block the other.

## Backwards plan

### Now → September 2026 — preparation
The window is not open yet. Everything that can be done in advance should be, because once
deadlines start landing there is no time to build assets.

- [ ] **ATS-safe CV built** from the template (`05-career-context/cv-template-ats.md`)
- [ ] **Shortlist programmes and scholarship schemes** — check each one's non-EU tuition
      and its age/experience criteria, which is where mid-career candidates get filtered out
- [ ] **Check entry requirements early** — English test (her C2 may still need proof),
      degree transcripts from Helwan, certified translations, referees. Transcripts from a
      2003 degree can take weeks to obtain; this is the classic late-discovered blocker
- [ ] **Case studies translated** for EU readers (`case-study-translator`)
- [ ] **Identify non-sponsor permit routes** — the highest-leverage unknown for Route A
- [ ] German continues — not on the critical path, but compounding

### October 2026 → February 2027 — the application window
- Applications open. **Submit early**, not at the deadline — some schemes assess on a
  rolling basis and funded places go first.
- Motivation letters per programme, to the stated word count exactly.
- Route A applications run in parallel and continuously.

### March → June 2027 — decisions and permits
- Offers and scholarship results.
- Visa/residence permit processing — **budget one to four months** depending on country.
  This is routinely underestimated and it is the step that slips departures.
- Notice period at WeDo. Only at this stage does the confidentiality rule relax, and only
  once something is signed.

### Aug–Sept 2027 — departure

## What this changes about priorities

The pressure is on **preparation**, not searching. Leads without a finished CV, translated
case studies and gathered documents cannot be converted when the window opens.

Concretely, between now and October: build the assets. From October: apply hard.
