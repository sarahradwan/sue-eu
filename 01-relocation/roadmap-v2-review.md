## ⛔ 1. It corrected a correct number with a wrong one

v2 claims:

> "The current official 2026 figure is **€2,442 per month**, based on 200% of the €1,221
> monthly SMI. The earlier €2,849 figure appears to have confused the dollar approximation
> with the euro threshold."

**Both halves of that are wrong. €2,849 is right.**

Spain's 2026 SMI is **€1,221/month paid in 14 instalments**, which is **€17,094 per year**
under Royal Decree 126/2026. The DNV test is 200% of the *annual* SMI:

**€17,094 × 2 = €34,188/year ÷ 12 = €2,849/month.**

([Jobbatical](https://www.jobbatical.com/blog/spain-digital-nomad-visa-income-smi-increase) ·
[Vissumlex](https://www.vissumlex.com/en/post/spain-digital-nomad-visa-requirements-2026-income-calculation-based-on-smi))

v2 multiplied the monthly figure by two and got €2,442, which silently drops the two extra
SMI instalments. And the diagnosis is demonstrably false: €2,849 × 12 = €34,188, a clean
euro figure matching the savings limb already in the vault. Nothing to do with dollars.

**Keep €2,849/month and €34,188/year.** More importantly, this is a "correction" delivered
with confidence and a wrong rationale, so **the other unsourced corrections in v2 deserve
the same scepticism.**

## ⚠️ 2. The Egyptian house sale — not in this vault, and it pauses two routes

v2 states:

> "The prior roadmap incorrectly treated the projected €30,600 by May 2027 as available

**There is no Egyptian house anywhere in this vault.** The €30,600 in
`08-uae-income/README.md` is derived from **salary saving**: at an AED 24,000/month package
against a mid burn, saving AED 13,000/month from August 2026 to May 2027. It is a savings
projection from income, not property proceeds.

So one of two things is true, and they lead opposite ways:

| If | Then |
|---|---|
| **A house sale is real** and Sara mentioned it elsewhere | v2 is right that it is a contingency, but it is **separate from** the €30,600, which stands on its own as salary saving. Germany and Austria should **not** be paused on the house |

**Sara needs to answer this.** It is the single highest-impact open question in the document,
because it decides whether the capital-enabled routes are prepared or shelved.

**What is true either way:** a projection is not a bank balance. `pipeline-scholarships.md`
already caught this error once with a projected gratuity. Do not open a blocked account or
commit to a self-funded plan until money is actually in an account.

## ✅ 3. The genuinely valuable new finding: Spanish Social Security

v2 reports that a UAE employer would need to **register with and contribute to Spanish
Social Security**, because there is no UAE–Spain social-security coordination agreement.
*Unverified here, but plausible and consistent with how Spain treats non-agreement countries.*

If correct, this **changes the shape of Route A2 substantially.** The vault has treated A2's
hard part as finding remote work. v2 identifies a harder gate: the employer must actively
agree to register as a foreign contributor in a country they do not operate in. That is a
real administrative ask, and most Gulf employers would decline it.

**This deserves verification before A2 keeps its current weight in the plan.** It does not
kill the route: freelance and self-employed applicants handle their own contributions, which
`08-uae-income/README.md` already identifies as the option that funds the move and builds a
route simultaneously. **Self-employed A2 may now be materially stronger than employed A2.**
