# Eligibility filter

Every lead is tested against this before it reaches Sara. A lead that fails is noise.
This file is the single definition of "qualifies" — if the criteria change, change them
here, not in individual notes.

## The two routes

A lead qualifies if it satisfies **Route A** or **Route B**. Nothing else counts.

### Route A — Employment

- Role in an EU country, and
- Employer **sponsors the visa / work permit**, or the route is one Sara can access
  without an employer sponsor (see permit notes below).

Explicit sponsorship language is required. "International team", "we welcome applicants
worldwide" and similar are *not* sponsorship. If sponsorship is unstated, the lead is
**unverified** — record it as such, never present it as qualifying.

### Route B — Funded study

Any one of:

- **Fully funded** scholarship (tuition + stipend), or
- Scholarship covering **first year**, or
- Scholarship covering **housing + some food**, or
- Tuition **under €2,000/year** with a viable living-cost plan.

Programme must lead to a residence permit, and ideally to post-study work rights.

## Countries

**Preferred:** Netherlands · Belgium · Austria

**Fully acceptable:** France · Spain · Ireland · Portugal · **any other EU country**

Do not narrow to the preferred three. The route matters more than the country. Rank by
strength of route first, country preference second.

## Hard constraints

| Constraint | Status |
|---|---|
| Current location | Abu Dhabi, UAE — applying from outside the EU |
| Right to work in EU | None currently |
| German | A1 — cannot carry a German-language role or degree |
| English | C2 — English-taught roles and programmes are the realistic field |
| Arabic | Native — an asset, not a limit |
| Tuition ceiling (Route B) | €2,000/year unless scholarship covers it |
| Field | Brand / creative direction / design / marketing / strategy |

## Automatic disqualifiers

- No visa sponsorship **and** not a funded-study route.
- Requires German (or another language) above her current level.
- Tuition above €2,000/year with no scholarship covering it.
- Non-EU country. (Note: UK and Switzerland are **not** EU — flag separately, do not
  file as qualifying without asking.)
- Unpaid, or salary below realistic living cost for the city.
- Deadline already passed.

## Every lead must record

1. Route (A or B)
2. Country and city
3. **Deadline** — the scarce resource. Flag anything inside 60 days.
4. Sponsorship / funding status: **confirmed** or **unverified**
5. Language of work or instruction
6. Source URL
7. Fit against her profile — what in the CV maps to it
8. Verdict: qualifies / fails / needs checking

## Notes on permits worth researching

Tracked in `country-notes.md` as research completes — several EU countries have routes
that do not depend on an employer sponsor (job-seeker visas, orientation-year permits
for recent graduates, self-employment and talent routes). These widen Route A
considerably and should be researched properly rather than assumed.
