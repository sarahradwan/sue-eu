## One dependency to keep visible

`pipeline-jobs.md` records that the **Nuffic credential evaluation cannot start until the
Egyptian documents are legalised**, and that the Blue Card cannot be applied for without it.
Budget six to ten weeks for legalisation, then Nuffic on top.

So A1 leads found now may not be convertible for months. That is an argument for starting
the legalisation chain, not for pausing the search: the roles have to be identified before
the paperwork is worth chasing, and one legalisation effort serves both routes.

**A2 is the channel that can move while documents are in transit**, because Spain accepts
three-plus years of professional experience in place of the degree limb. Weight it
accordingly.
