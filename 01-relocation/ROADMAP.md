## ⚠️ Three gaps to correct before this is fully operational

v3 is sound in structure. These are allocation and emphasis problems, not errors.

### 1. The weekly time split funds a closed route ahead of live ones

Section 7 allocates **2–3 hours a week to German**, the largest single block in a 6–8 hour
week, roughly a third of all effort. Meanwhile **IELTS and document administration share
45–60 minutes.**

But **Germany is closed**, and none of the three live A1 lanes needs German. Brussels,
Netherlands and Sweden all run on English. And A2 to working fluency inside ten months was
never realistic anyway.

**IELTS and documents are on the critical path of the live routes. German is optionality for
a route that is shut.** The split should be roughly inverted until IELTS is sat and the
documents are legalised.

*German is still worth continuing at a maintenance level. It is a genuine long-term asset
and dropping it entirely would be a mistake. It should simply not outrank the things with
deadlines.*

### 2. Document legalisation is understated as "inventory"

v3 asks Sara to *"inventory degree, transcript, passport."* That is not the task.

`pipeline-jobs.md` establishes that **Nuffic credential evaluation cannot begin until the
Egyptian documents are legalised**, and the Dutch Blue Card cannot be applied for without
Nuffic. Egypt is not an Apostille country: the chain runs Egyptian Ministry of Education or
Supreme Council of Universities → Egyptian MoFA → destination embassy. **Six to ten weeks,
then Nuffic on top.**

**This is the longest lead time in the entire plan and it gates the Netherlands lane
outright.** Having the documents in a folder is not the same as having them legalised.
It belongs in section 8 as a dated action, not as an inventory line.

### 3. The IELTS deadline is not given its weight

v3 mentions *"resolve English-test requirements"* in passing. In fact **Swedish admission
closes mid-January 2027**, Sara has never sat IELTS, and Route B is one of only two live
routes. A resit needs weeks.

The IELTS sitting is a **hard dependency of a live route with a fixed external deadline.**
It should sit alongside the November SI programme gate, not inside an administration block.
