# German — vocabulary

Spaced repetition. New words go in with the date added; resurface at roughly
1 day → 3 days → 1 week → 2 weeks → 1 month. A recall failure resets to the start.

**Nouns always with their article** — `das Haus`, never `Haus`. A noun learned without its
gender has to be learned twice.

Anchor vocabulary to her actual life: design, brand, agency work, everyday Abu Dhabi,
travel. Words she will reuse stick; textbook vocabulary does not.
