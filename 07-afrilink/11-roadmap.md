# 11. Roadmap

Sequenced against the constraints that actually bind: the IELTS sitting, the attestation chain,
the Swedish application window, and the fact that **nothing goes on a public register or her
personal LinkedIn until she has resigned.**

**The organising principle:** Afrilink is the only commitment in the vault with no external
deadline. Everything else — IELTS, attestation, 15 Jan, the SI window — has a date that costs a
year if missed. So Afrilink's cadence is set by what is left over, and it deliberately runs at
its lightest through the window where the relocation is won or lost.

---

## The shape of it

| Phase | When | What | Sara's load |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0 — Decide** | Now → Sept 2026 | Answer the blocking questions. Build nothing | **2–4h/month** |
| **1 — Quiet build** | Oct 2026 → Feb 2027 | Landing page, applications open, batch content. **No public cadence** | **4–6h/month** |
| **2 — Soft launch** | Mar → Jul 2027 | Page live, bi-weekly spotlights, freelancers recruited | **9–17h/month** |
| **3 — Relocate & register** | Aug → Dec 2027 | Resign, register the entity, first gathering, Sara goes public | Variable |
| **4 — Monetise** | 2028 | Commission, sponsorship, grants. Weekly cadence if the buffer holds | — |

**Note the inversion.** The heaviest work happens *after* the relocation is secured, not before.
That is the opposite of the original brief, and it is the single most important structural
change in this whole evaluation.

## Phase 0 — Decide · now → Sept 2026

**Build nothing. Answer things.** IELTS and attestation own this period.

| # | Action | Why it blocks | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Read the WeDo contract** — outside work, conflict of interest, non-compete | Determines whether Sara can be involved at all, and how visibly | Sara — 20 min |
| 2 | **Establish Hadeer's exact visa type** | Determines whether the venture has a clean legal front person | Hadeer — one message |
| 3 | **Verify the Belgian two-resident-members rule** | Decides Belgium vs Netherlands, and therefore cost and timeline | Sara / lawyer |
| 4 | **Decide the name.** EUIPO + WIPO + domain + handle clearance | Everything downstream carries it. Renaming later is expensive | Sara + Hadeer |
| 5 | **Recruit 2–3 Sub-Saharan African co-founders or advisers** | Answers the identity question before it becomes the criticism | Sara + Hadeer |
| 6 | Confirm Ahmed's commitment and a named fallback editor | He is on the critical path of a recurring engine | Sara |

**Exit criteria — all six answered.** Do not start Phase 1 with any of them open.

🔴 **Absolute priority: IELTS.** Book in the first half of August, sit the last week of
September. A late sitting collapses three retake windows into one. **If Afrilink work is
competing with IELTS preparation in September, Afrilink stops.** No exceptions — that decision
is made here, calmly, rather than in September under pressure.

## Phase 1 — Quiet build · Oct 2026 → Feb 2027

**The application window. This phase is deliberately the lightest thing in the plan.**

Hard dates it must not disturb:

| Date | What |
|---|---|
| 16 Oct 2026 | universityadmissions.se opens |
| **15 Jan 2027** | **Swedish application deadline** |
| 1 Feb 2027 | Supporting documents backstop |
| **~9–25 Feb 2027** | **SI Global Professionals scholarship portal** *(inferred from the 2026 cycle — verify)* |

### What gets built

- **Landing page + application form.** Proof of trading, three GDPR consents (directory,
  newsletter, likeness) built in from record one
- **Applications open** — batched intake, quarterly rounds
- **Target: 15–25 verified founding members**
- **Batch-produce 6–8 spotlights.** Produced, not published. This is the buffer that makes
  Phase 2 survivable
- Quiet outreach: EU African diaspora organisations, first freelancer conversations

### What does NOT happen

- ❌ No public launch, no publishing cadence, no announcements
- ❌ No entity registration
- ❌ Nothing on Sara's personal LinkedIn
- ❌ No live sessions

**Exit criteria — the demand gate.** Before Phase 2 begins:

| Signal | Reading |
|---|---|
| **25+ applications, 15+ verified** | Strong. Proceed |
| **12–24 applications** | Viable. Proceed cautiously |
| 6–11 applications | Weak. Extend Phase 1, do not launch |
| **<6 applications in 90 days** | 🛑 **Stop.** The demand is not there. Reassess the audience definition before spending more |

⚠️ **That last row is the point of having a roadmap.** A plan without a stop condition is a wish
list. Ninety days and fewer than six applications means the segment assumption in the canvas is
wrong, and the correct response is to find out why — not to try harder.

## Phase 2 — Soft launch · Mar → Jul 2027

**Applications submitted. IELTS done. Attestation done. Real capacity returns.**

- **Afrilink LinkedIn Page goes live**
- **Bi-weekly spotlights** from the buffer. Weekly only once a four-week buffer has held for two
  months
- **Monthly interview** — record, AI-transcribe, Ahmed edits
- **Async Q&A** on the page. Still no live sessions
- **Monthly free consultation slot**, delivered by a network freelancer — the mechanism that
  recruits the freelancer side
- **Monthly newsletter** carrying network activity, not curated news
- Target: **50–80 verified members**

**Still off-limits:** entity registration, Sara's personal LinkedIn, any named public founder
role for Sara.

**Publishing rule, in force from day one of this phase:**

> **Never publish from an empty queue. Hold a four-week buffer. When it drops below two weeks,
> that is the signal to batch — not the week the post is due.**

**Exit criteria:** 50+ verified members · 8+ spotlights published · 3+ freelancers signed up ·
buffer held for two consecutive months.

## Phase 3 — Relocate and register · Aug → Dec 2027

**The hinge. Confidentiality lifts only once something is signed.**

Order matters here:

1. **Offer or scholarship confirmed** → notice served at WeDo
2. **Only then** does the confidentiality constraint lift (`01-relocation/timeline-to-2027.md`
   is explicit: only once something is signed)
3. **Sara becomes a public founder** — personal LinkedIn unlocked, announcement, the founder
   story becomes publishable
4. **Register the entity** — Netherlands if the Belgian residency rule bites; Belgium if not, or
   once two Belgium-resident members exist. €266–€1,575
5. **First in-person gathering** — free, unticketed, invitation-based. The momentum moment she
   wanted, now in the right country and at the right time
6. Target: **100+ members**

⚠️ **Do not register before step 1.** The Moniteur Belge publishes founders' names, professions
and home addresses; the Dutch KVK register is public. Registering while employed puts
*"Sara Radwan, Creative Director, Abu Dhabi, founder of a Belgian non-profit for EU women
founders"* on a permanent public record. That cannot be undone.

**If the 2027 route does not land:** Phase 3 slips, Phase 2 continues. **That is survivable and
it is not failure.** By February 2028 Afrilink would be ~18 months old with a real track record —
which `03-relocation-interaction.md` judged a materially stronger scholarship application than
2027's. The roadmap is deliberately built so the fallback is better than the primary, not worse.

## The gates, in one place

Each phase has one question that must be answered *yes* to proceed:

| Gate | Question | If no |
|---|---|---|
| **0 → 1** | Is Sara legally clear, and is there a front person? | Restructure so Hadeer or an EU co-founder fronts it |
| **1 → 2** | **Did 12+ founders apply in 90 days?** | 🛑 Stop and reassess the audience |
| **2 → 3** | Is something signed at WeDo? | Stay in Phase 2. Do not register |
| **3 → 4** | Is the entity registered with 100+ members? | Stay in Phase 3 |

---

## What this roadmap protects

Read as a whole, the sequencing does three things:

1. **The relocation comes first.** Nothing in Phases 0–2 can damage the IELTS sitting, the
   attestation chain or the application window. That was the central objection in this
   evaluation and the roadmap is built to answer it.
2. **Nothing public until something is signed.** No register entry, no personal LinkedIn, no
   founder announcement while her employer can still act on it.
3. **The fallback is stronger than the primary.** If 2027 does not land, 2028 has an 18-month-old
   organisation behind it.

**The cost of this sequencing is that Afrilink grows slowly for a year.** That is the trade
being made deliberately: a slower venture in exchange for an uncompromised relocation. Given
that the relocation is the goal and Afrilink is the asset, that is the right way round.
