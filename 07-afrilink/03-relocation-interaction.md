# 3. The relocation question, both ways

The brief's optimistic case is **structurally correct and materially overstated**. Founding an
organisation is genuinely the evidence SI Global Professionals selects on. But Sara is not
short of that evidence, and the version of Afrilink that exists by the application date is too
young to add much.

The pessimistic case is **real but solvable** — and solvable in a way that improves the
venture rather than compromising it.

---

## First: the load-bearing claim checks out

The brief rests on SI Global Professionals being her strongest route. That claim survives
verification.

| Claim | Status |
|---|---|
| Egypt is an eligible country | **Search-verified.** 34 eligible countries "including four Arab nations: Egypt, Iraq, Jordan, and Morocco" ([Al-Fanar Media](https://al-fanarmedia.org/scholarships/swedish-institute-scholarships-for-global-professionals-2/), [Scholars4Dev](https://www.scholars4dev.com/5295/sweden-scholarships-for-international-students-from-developing-countries/)) |
| Weighs leadership and work experience | **Search-verified.** Requires ≥3,000 documented work hours and "clear evidence of leadership potential" — explicitly "from a current or previous employer **or from civil society engagement**" ([SI](https://si.se/en/apply/scholarships/swedish-institute-scholarships-for-global-professionals/)) |
| No GPA gate | **Consistent with sources** — no GPA threshold appears in any eligibility summary found. Absence of evidence; treat as *probable, not confirmed* |
| Fully funded | **Search-verified.** Full tuition, SEK 12,000/month, SEK 15,000 travel grant |

The phrase **"or from civil society engagement"** is the brief's whole thesis in the scheme's
own words. Afrilink would count. That much is real.

### ⚠️ Two things this turned up that are not in her timeline files

**1. SISGP is a two-stage application with a second deadline in February.**
For the 2026 cycle: programme applications closed **15 January 2026**, then the SI scholarship
portal opened separately **9–25 February 2026** ([Scholarship Roar](https://scholarshiproar.com/swedish-institute-scholarships-for-global-professionals-sisgp/),

By analogy the 2027 cycle would run **~9–25 February 2027** — *inferred from the 2026 pattern,

`01-relocation/timeline-to-2027.md` and `06-ielts/timeline-and-booking.md` both treat 15 Jan
2027 as the end of the crunch. **For this route it is not.** There is a second, separate,
two-week window roughly a month later, and it is the window where the leadership essay — the
part Afrilink would feed — actually gets written.

**2. The 3,000 hours must come from "a maximum of three employers."**
([Al-Fanar Media](https://al-fanarmedia.org/scholarships/swedish-institute-scholarships-for-global-professionals-2/))
Sara clears 3,000 hours many times over, so the hours are not the risk. The *three-employer
cap* over a 22-year career is worth checking against how the form is structured — it is the
kind of clause that quietly complicates a long CV.

## The case FOR — real, but smaller than it looks

**Where the brief is right:** "expand this organisation into European African-diaspora
communities" is a genuine answer to *why now, and what will you do with it*. Year 3 of the plan
already names the Netherlands, France, Germany and Belgium. That is a coherent arc, not a
retrofit, and it is verifiable — a panel can look it up.

**Where the brief overstates it — the marginal-value problem.**

The question is not *"does founding an organisation help?"* It is *"how much does it add **to
Sara specifically**, given what is already on her application?"*

From `05-career-context/cv-source-of-truth.md` and `01-relocation/eligibility-filter.md`, she
already brings:

- 22 years, director level, multidisciplinary team leadership
- A €500k national curriculum programme — 100+ books, 5 subjects, 3 scripts
- Brand architecture across four countries
- Minister-level institutional stakeholders
- **A previously co-founded company (Social Dar)** — founder evidence she already owns
- AI governance written into client brand systems
- CIM Level 7 in progress

Set against that, what does a five-month-old community group with four events and perhaps 60
WhatsApp members add to a leadership assessment?

**Honestly: very little.** She is not a candidate whose file is thin on leadership and needs
padding. She is a candidate with 22 years of it. The scheme asks for evidence of leadership
potential; she is comfortably past the bar before Afrilink exists.

**The marginal value is low, and the marginal cost — per `02-calendar-collision.md` — is the
IELTS score and the quality of the motivation letter.** Trading application *quality* for
application *material* she does not need is a bad trade. That is the core finding of this
section.

## Would a panel find it credible, or contrived?

Asked for rigour rather than optimism, so: **it depends almost entirely on one thing, and it
is not effort.**

**What reads as contrived:**
- Founded ~5 months before the application, in the applicant's stated field of aspiration
- A Year-3 plan naming exactly the four countries the applicant wants to move to
- No track record — few events, no outcomes, no independent verification
- The applicant departing the country of founding within a year of the award

Panels see a great deal of this. Scholarship-shaped extracurriculars founded in the months
before a deadline are a recognised pattern, and assessors are calibrated for it. **A
five-month-old organisation with four events, presented as the centrepiece of a leadership
narrative, invites the exact scepticism she cannot afford.**

**What reads as credible:**
- 18+ months of operation with visible consistency
- Evidence it functions **without her** — a co-founder running it, other people leading
- Concrete outcomes: numbers, testimonials, partnerships, named collaborators
- A handover already executed, not merely promised
- Honest framing: *"I built this, I handed it over, here is what it does now"*

The discriminator is **not how hard she worked. It is whether the organisation survives her.**
An organisation that outlives its founder's departure is genuine institution-building. One that
collapses when she leaves was a personal project, and a panel that asks one follow-up question
will find out which.

**This is the same test as the ethical question below.** They are not two problems. Building a
handover that protects the members is precisely what makes the application credible. That
alignment is the most useful thing in this document — it means she does not have to choose
between doing right by the community and doing right by the application.

## The case AGAINST — founding and leaving

Direct answer to the direct question: **founding a community and leaving 12–18 months in is
fine if the handover is designed from the start, and a genuine harm if it is not.**

Communities are not products. People reorganise their weeks around them, form working
relationships through them, and take professional risks on the strength of them. `01-legal-uae.md`
notes the demographic reality: an African women's professional community in the UAE serves
people whose own residency is employer-tied and often precarious. A group that promises
continuity and then dissolves does real damage to people with fewer options than Sara has.

**What responsible looks like:**

| Element | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Co-founder independence | Hadeer must be able to run it alone — proven by her actually doing so, not by agreement |
| No founder dependency | Sara's name, face and network cannot be the reason people attend |
| Distributed leadership | The "10–15 highly engaged members" in the brief are not just ambassadors; they are the succession plan |
| Legal continuity | If a licence exists it must be in a name that stays (`01-legal-uae.md` Option B) |
| Honest communication | Members told there is a founding team and a transition, not discovering it |

**When must it start? Before the launch, not before the departure.**

If Sara leaves in mid-2027 and the venture launches September 2026, a "handover starting
early 2027" means she is transitioning out at month five — barely past founding. That is not a
handover; it is an abandonment with a schedule.

**The only coherent design is one where Sara is never the indispensable person.** Which means
Hadeer fronts it from day one. Which is `01-legal-uae.md` Option B, and `02-calendar-collision.md`'s
"Sara advising ≤8h/month" version.

**Three independent lines of analysis have now converged on the same structure.** That is the
strongest signal in this evaluation.

## Timing: does it actually work?

| Launch | State at Feb 2027 essay | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **Sept 2026** | ~5 months, 4–5 events | Usable, thin, and paid for with IELTS prep and letter quality. **Bad trade** |
| **March 2027** | Not yet launched | Nothing for the 2027 application — but nothing lost either |
| **Sept 2026, private and quarterly** | ~6 months, 2 gatherings, honest | Modest, true, costs almost nothing |

**The uncomfortable conclusion: for a January–February 2027 application, Afrilink cannot be
made strong enough to matter, at any effort level that does not damage the application it is
meant to strengthen.** There is no version of this that is both substantial and cheap by
February 2027. The arithmetic does not allow it.

### Where it genuinely does pay off

**If the 2027 cycle does not land**, a 2028 application is transformed. By February 2028
Afrilink would be ~18 months old with a real track record, a completed handover, and a founder
who built an institution that outlived her presence. That is a materially stronger file than
2027's.

That is the honest framing: **Afrilink is a strong 2028 asset and a weak 2027 one.** Given the
2027 cycle is the priority and 2028 is the fallback, building it *slowly* now hedges the
fallback without taxing the primary.

Note the useful asymmetry — the slow, private, Hadeer-led version is the best option under
*both* scenarios. It costs the 2027 application nothing, and it is the only version that has
matured into something by 2028.

---

## Verdict on Q3

**For:** the scheme's own criteria name civil society leadership; Egypt is eligible; the
Europe-expansion narrative is coherent and verifiable. All real.

**Against:** she already exceeds the leadership bar; a five-month-old venture reads as
contrived rather than convincing; and the preparation it displaces is exactly what determines
whether the application is any good.

**Resolution:** build it in the form that survives her — slowly, privately, with Hadeer
leading. That version costs the 2027 application nothing, materially strengthens a 2028 one,
and is the only version that treats the members honestly.

**Do not build Afrilink for the scholarship. Build it so it survives, and let the scholarship
benefit if the timing allows.** Applications built backwards from what a panel wants to hear
are the ones panels detect.
