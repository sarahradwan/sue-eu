># The number

Sara asked: if a lottery paid exactly enough to clear the debts, move, live comfortably, and
work or start a company, what is that figure?

Worth answering seriously. It reveals the size of the actual gap, and the gap is smaller than
it feels.

**Debt confirmed by Sara, 12 Aug 2026: AED 50,000, which is about €11,765 at AED 4.25 to the
euro.**

## ✅ The answer

| Tier | Euro | Dirham |
|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1 — clean, debt-free, safe landing** | **€46,300** | **AED 197,000** |
| **Tier 1 with employer relocation support** | **€34,800** | **AED 148,000** |
| Tier 2 — secure, two years of runway | €100,300 | AED 426,000 |
| Tier 3 — plus start the company | €161,800 | AED 688,000 |
| Tier 4 — plus own the home outright | €541,800 | AED 2,302,000 |
| Tier 5 — never work again | ~€1.31m | ~AED 5.6m |

**The number is €46,300. Roughly AED 197,000.**

That is the whole distance between where she is now and a safe, debt-free landing in Europe
with six months of cushion.

**And it drops to about €35,000 if an employer pays relocation**, which is a normal term for
an international hire and costs her nothing to ask for.

**A Sweden SI award would cover a large part of what remains**, since it pays tuition, living
costs, insurance and travel. Debt clearance would be the main item left.

---

All figures 2026, euros, one person, no dependants. Netherlands used as the base because it
is the most expensive of the live lanes. Brussels runs roughly 20 to 25 percent cheaper.

## The honest headline

**The life-changing number is Tier 1, around €35,000 plus the debt.** Not a million. Not
half a million.

That is the entire distance between the current position, where a single unexpected expense
derails the plan, and a position where the move is safe.

**Why it matters strategically:** €35,000 plus debt is not a lottery, it is a savings and
income problem with a time horizon. Tier 4 and Tier 5 genuinely require luck. Tier 1 does
not.

**And the plan already routes around most of it.** A sponsored employer covers the permit
and often relocation. A fully funded scholarship covers tuition, living, insurance and
travel. Both were chosen precisely because they do not need capital. What the lottery would
buy is not the route, it is the **safety margin around the route**, and the freedom to say no.

---

## What to do with this

1. **Supply D.** Without it every figure is incomplete, and the debt is likely the single
   largest item.
2. **Treat €35,000 + D as the real target**, not €15,000. `savings-target.md` sets €15,000,
   which covers a lean move but not a safe one.
3. **Negotiate relocation support in every offer.** An employer paying flights, shipping and
   a deposit removes roughly €11,500 from Tier 1 without her saving a cent.
4. **Sweden SI, if awarded, removes most of Tier 1 by itself.** Tuition, living costs,
   insurance and travel. That is why it is not a hedge.
