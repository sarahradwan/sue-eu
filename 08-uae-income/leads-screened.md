### Why it likely fails her own number

`market-rate.md`: **AED 23,000–25,000 is a stretch against agency data and unremarkable against
corporate data.** ERI models a *senior, 8+ year* agency Creative Director in Dubai at
~AED 17,000/month. **An ACD at a network agency, screened against an 8–12 year band, is
unlikely to reach AED 25,000.**

### ⛔ And it fails condition 2 outright

**On-site, Dubai.** She lives in Abu Dhabi. UAE rent runs on annual cheques — a new Dubai
tenancy means **AED 25,000–60,000 up front**, plus agency fee, deposit, and a likely two-month
penalty to break the Abu Dhabi lease. **That is the entire €15,000 savings target, spent in
month one.** The alternative is roughly three hours a day commuting, taken out of exactly the
evenings the EU applications, CIM, German and the freelance track need.

**Condition 2 exists because of this posting.** It was the gap in the original framework.

### ⚠️ The merger risk

**Omnicom is absorbing IPG now**, and MENA is specifically flagged as the contested part — MCN
overlaps heavily with Omnicom's existing MENA agencies (Impact BBDO, TBWA\RAAD, DDB).
[Communicate Online](https://communicateonline.me/news/media/as-omnicom-absorbs-ipg-all-eyes-turn-to-the-mena-power-puzzle/)

Two consequences: a newly hired ACD at a legacy-IPG agency during integration is the person
consolidation catches; **and internal mobility freezes during a merger** — which removes the
internal job board, the one thing that justified an EU-office employer on her timeline at all.

[momentumww.com/office/dubai](https://www.momentumww.com/office/dubai/) or find the named hirer

### How to play it

**Apply this week, off LinkedIn, and put the number in early.** Not late. If Momentum's band is
AED 18,000 this is a no, and she should learn that in week one rather than week six — the point
of the application is as much the salary information as the job.

**Do not ask permission first.** `pipeline-jobs.md`, corrected 26 July: asking *"would you
consider someone with 22 years against an 8–12 band?"* gives a recruiter a free ten-second way
to filter her out. Make them evaluate the portfolio.

**Keep the two questions apart:** the role is worth applying for; the job is probably not worth
taking. If it converts, the Dubai housing arithmetic decides, not the title.
