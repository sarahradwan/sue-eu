## Why this variant is different from every EU variant — and it inverts one rule

Three deliberate reversals of the EU approach:

**1. Name the clients. All of them.**

`case-study-translator` exists because *"RTA means nothing in Rotterdam"*, and every EU variant
anonymises her work. **In the UAE that logic runs backwards.** The UAE Ministry of Education,
Dubai Municipality, Dubai's Roads and Transport Authority and Dubai Sports Council are
immediately recognised, and being trusted with them is the credential. **This is the one market
where her client list is an asset rather than an explanation.**

**2. Lead with brand and marketing, not with creative.**

`market-rate.md` found an ~18% premium on the marketing title over the creative title in the
same UAE dataset — the same conclusion `pipeline-jobs.md` reached for Europe on 26 July. **CIM
Level 7 goes into the summary, not the footnotes.**

**3. Arabic is a stated professional capability, not a language-section line.**

Bilingual Arabic and English brand systems are a working requirement across the UAE
institutional and consumer market, and she has built them at national scale across three
scripts. In Europe that is an unusual differentiator. Here it is a qualification.

**No sponsorship framing anywhere.** She holds UAE residency. Every EU variant has to address
right to work; this one does not, which frees a paragraph.
