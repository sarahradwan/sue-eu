## Why a move earns so little: the arithmetic

A job change is not a pay rise from today. It is a pay rise from whenever it actually starts,
running only until she leaves.

**Realistic sequence, starting now:**

| Step | When |
|---|---|
| Search, recruiter conversations, approaches | Aug–Oct 2026 |
| Offer | Nov 2026 |
| Notice at WeDo — 30 to 90 days, Article 43 | Nov 2026 – Jan 2027 |
| **Start the new job** | **Jan–Feb 2027** |
| Resign again, to depart mid-2027 | **May–Jun 2027** |

**That is three to five months in the new job.** At a good uplift of AED 5,000/month:

| | |
|---|---|
| Gross gain, 4 months × AED 5,000 | **AED 20,000 ≈ €4,700** |
| Less: two months of disrupted saving during search and transition | −AED 3,200 to −AED 6,400 |
| Less: gratuity forfeited (below) | −AED 5,000 to −AED 8,000 |
| Less: residence visa cancellation and reissue | −AED 1,000 to −AED 3,000 **[ESTIMATE]** |
| **Net** | **≈ AED 12,000–15,000 · €2,800–3,500** |

**Under a quarter of the target — for the largest single disruption available to her.**

And the sequence assumes everything lands on time. If the offer slips to December, the new job
starts in March and the gain halves again.

## The contract mechanics, checked

### Notice period — Article 43

Either party may terminate for a legitimate reason with written notice; the contract continues
during the notice period, which is **not less than 30 days and not more than 90**. Full wage is
payable throughout. Failing to serve it means compensating the other party for the unserved
days — deductible from the final settlement.
[u.ae, official](https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/jobs/employment-in-the-private-sector/job-offers-and-work-permits-and-contracts/terminating-employment-contracts) ·
[Yomly](https://www.yomly.com/notice-period-in-uae/)

**Action: read her WeDo contract for the actual figure.** 30 days and 90 days are very different
plans — 90 days means an offer accepted in November does not start until February.

### Probation — Article 9, and this one is a genuine trap

Probation may not exceed **six months**. During it:

- Leaving to join **another UAE employer**: **one month's notice**, and **the new employer must
  compensate the previous employer's recruitment costs** unless otherwise agreed.
- Leaving **the UAE entirely**: **14 days' notice**.
  [HRSG](https://uae.hrsgonline.com/resources/probation-period-in-uae-meaning-rules-employee-rights-under-labour-law-2026/)

**Why it bites here:** if she starts a new job in February 2027 with six months' probation, she
is still on probation in June 2027 when she wants to leave. The 14-day exit for leaving the
country is actually helpful — but she would be resigning from a job she has held for four
months, on probation, which is the worst possible entry in a CV she is simultaneously sending
to European employers who already have to be persuaded to sponsor her.

**If she does move, negotiate probation down to three months.** It is negotiable at senior level
and it is worth more than a small salary difference.

### Labour ban — real, but avoidable

Not automatic. MoHRE may impose a work-permit ban of **up to one year**, and it follows an
**employer complaint** — typically for work abandonment or leaving without serving notice. MoHRE
investigates before imposing anything, and the ban can be lifted with the original employer's
approval.
[EGSH](https://egsh.ae/insights/labour-ban-in-the-uae) ·
[UAE Expert Hub](https://www.uaeexperthub.com/uae-labour-ban-types-duration-removal/)

> **Serve the notice period in full and there is no ban.** This is not a meaningful risk for
> someone leaving properly. It is a risk only for someone who walks out.

**One related clause worth knowing:** if she leaves the UAE and returns **within three months**
on a new work permit, the new employer may be liable to compensate the previous employer's
recruitment costs. Irrelevant to a permanent move to Europe; relevant if any plan involves
leaving and coming back.

### Non-compete — Article 10

Permitted, capped at **two years** from contract expiry, and must be limited in time, place and
nature of work. Courts refuse to enforce clauses drawn too widely.
[Mayer Brown](https://www.mayerbrown.com/en/insights/publications/2024/07/restrictive-covenants-uae)

**Relevance to her:** essentially nil for a move to Europe — a UAE non-compete does not follow
her to Amsterdam. It matters if she moves to another **UAE agency serving the same government
clients**, which is precisely the Tier 3 category `target-employers.md` already tells her to
avoid. Moving in-house or to a differently-focused group sidesteps it.

### ⚠️ Training-cost clawback — the live question about CIM

Repayment is enforceable **only** for actual, documented costs directly related to training, and
**only proportionately to service given**.
[Legal Advice ME](https://legaladviceme.com/questions/102088/non-compete-training-repayment-clauses-valid-uae-labor-law)

> **Is WeDo paying for CIM Level 7?**
>
> If yes, resigning mid-programme could trigger a repayment claim, and the qualification is a
> documented, invoiced, datable cost — the exact shape a UAE court will enforce. A Level 7
> Postgraduate Diploma is not a small number.
>
> **This is in her contract and I cannot see it. It needs checking before any resignation,
> including the eventual one for Europe.** If WeDo is funding it, the repayment schedule may
> influence the departure date itself.

### Gratuity — smaller loss than it feels, and one hidden upside

**21 days of basic salary per year for the first five years.** Since 2 February 2022 an employee
who resigns after one year receives the **full** entitlement — the old reduction for resignation
was abolished. **Under one year of service: no gratuity at all.** Calculated on the **last basic
salary**, not the total package, and not on the average.
[Paci Finance](https://paci.ae/blogs/uae-end-of-service-gratuity/) ·
[Khaleej Times calculator](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/jobs/gratuity-calculator)

| Scenario | WeDo service | Gratuity from WeDo | New employer service | Gratuity from new employer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Stay** | Feb 2025 – Jun 2027 ≈ **2.4 yrs** | ~**50 days** of basic | — | — |
| **Move Feb 2027** | Feb 2025 – Jan 2027 ≈ **2.0 yrs** | ~**42 days** of basic | 4 months | **Zero** — under one year |

**Cost of moving: roughly 8 days of basic salary** — AED 4,800 if basic is AED 18,000, AED 3,200
if basic is AED 12,000. Real, but not decisive. The vault previously flagged the gratuity as
possibly smaller than expected; that holds, and it also means it is a small argument against
moving rather than a large one.

**The hidden upside sits on the other side of the ledger.** Gratuity is calculated on her
**last** basic salary. **A raise at WeDo before she leaves increases the gratuity too** — a
compounding benefit that a job change destroys, because the new employer's clock never reaches
one year.

### ⚠️ The residency risk nobody has flagged yet

Changing employer means cancelling and reissuing her UAE residence visa, with a gap in between.

Two things in this vault depend on continuous UAE residency:

1. **`pipeline-jobs.md`: she applies for the Spanish digital nomad visa at the Spanish mission
   in Abu Dhabi, because "UAE residents are explicitly eligible."** Her Egyptian passport rules
   out the in-Spain route entirely. **A break in UAE residency could break her eligibility to
   use that consulate at all.**
2. **The Egyptian legalisation chain** — Ministry of Education, Egyptian MoFA, destination
   embassy — is run remotely from Abu Dhabi and takes 6–10 weeks. It runs Aug–Dec 2026, exactly
   when a job change would disturb her residency status.

**A visa gap in that window could cost her a route and the longest lead time in the plan at the
same time.** Verify before accepting any offer that requires a cancel-and-reissue rather than a
straightforward transfer.

### Reference risk

`application-history.md` already rules out WeDo referees without her say-so, and directs
referees to Social Dar, the Ministry programme, DAIS and CPI. So a WeDo reference is not
load-bearing today.

**A new employer changes that for the worse.** It becomes her most recent role — the one every
European employer asks about — and she would be leaving it after four months. She cannot use
them as a referee, and their absence from her reference list is conspicuous in a way WeDo's is
not, because WeDo can be explained as a confidential search and four months cannot.

### Why C wins on the second axis, not just the first

`pipeline-jobs.md` on the Spain digital nomad visa: it **explicitly accepts freelancers and

**Freelance income started in September 2026 is nine months of provable recurring non-Spanish
income by May 2027 — three times the minimum, with documentation.** It funds the target and
constructs the fallback route simultaneously.

**Nothing else on this page does two jobs at once.**

## What she should actually do

### 1. Ask for the raise — September 2026, before budgets close

The argument writes itself, and none of it hints at leaving:

- **The title does not describe the job.** She is titled *Senior Art Director* while leading
  creative vision for government clients, presenting to ministers and owning senior client
  relationships. That is a Creative Director remit. **Ask about the title and the salary
  together** — it is the cleanest framing available and it reads as commitment, not exit.
- **12% government tender win rate contribution** — a commercial number, in a business that
  lives on tenders.
- **Benet7awel for Dubai Municipality** — a whole headquarters carried through a culture change.
- **AI capability commands a named salary premium in the UAE right now**
  ([Arabian Business](https://www.arabianbusiness.com/jobs/uae-salary-guide-2026-the-fastest-growing-sectors-top-paying-jobs-and-skills-employers-want-most)),
  and she leads AI adoption in creative production including AI usage guidelines written into

- **CIM Level 7 in progress** — and `stealth-positioning` confirms this is completely safe to
  raise publicly, let alone internally.
- **The market is moving 4.1% in 2026.** A flat salary is a real-terms cut.

**Target: +AED 4,000/month. Floor: +AED 2,500.** Even the floor beats the net gain from changing
jobs, for none of the cost.

### 2. Start the freelance track — September 2026

After the exclusivity and permit checks. Non-UAE clients only. One or two retained relationships

### 3. Cut burn — August 2026

### 4. Keep the job search open, but narrow

Not off — **narrow**. Only tier-1 employers from `target-employers.md`, only through the private
channels in `stealth-search-uae.md`. The point is no longer the salary; it is the European

sits inside the same company as a Dubai office.

## The five conditions — move only if ALL are true

1. **Uplift ≥ AED 6,000/month.** Below that the net does not survive the friction above.
2. **Tier-1 employer** on `target-employers.md` — a genuine European end, not just money.
3. **Start date on or before 1 November 2026**, so probation and ramp-up finish before the EU
   application window peaks.
4. **Contract clean:** probation ≤ 3 months · no training clawback · no relocation bond · notice
   ≤ 60 days · non-compete limited in scope.
5. **No break in UAE residency** — a transfer, not a cancel-and-reissue.

Any one failing and the answer is stay. **On today's date, condition 3 alone means an offer
would have to be agreed within roughly eight weeks**, which in practice means it happens only if
a tier-1 employer already has something open.

## 🔁 The framing that resolves the tension

**"Is moving jobs net positive?" has two different answers depending on one decision she has
already made and could revisit.**

| Departure target | Verdict on changing UAE jobs |
|---|---|
| **Mid-2027** (current plan) | **No.** Three to five months of uplift cannot repay the disruption, and it lands on the EU application window |
| **Mid-2028** | **Strongly yes.** Eighteen months at a tier-1 employer makes the intra-company transfer genuinely available, the savings target becomes comfortable rather than tight, and `target-employers.md` becomes a relocation strategy rather than a CV improvement |

The current plan is mid-2027 and this document assumes it. **But the case for changing jobs is
really a case for a different timeline**, and she should see that clearly rather than conclude
the target list was wrong. It is not wrong. It is early.
