## The four tailoring decisions

**1. Marketing vocabulary, not creative vocabulary — throughout.**
`market-rate.md` found an ~18% premium on the marketing title over the creative title in the
same UAE dataset, and `pipeline-jobs.md` found her CIM has gone unused across every application.
This posting is a marketing role. The header drops *Creative Leadership* for **Brand Management
and Brand Governance**, and the summary opens on brand custodianship rather than creative
direction.

**2. Brand custodianship leads, because it is their first accountability.**
*"Act as brand custodian across the group, reviewing and providing clear brand guidance and
approvals on all brand-facing assets"* and *"develop and evolve brand guidelines and
templates."* That is her single strongest documented discipline — brand guidelines, design
systems, and the standards that let other people apply an identity without it drifting. It sits
mid-CV in the master and leads here.

**3. CIM Level 7 is promoted with its syllabus spelled out.**
Their accountabilities read as the CIM syllabus: customer insight, agency briefing, campaign
development and **post-campaign evaluation**, brand performance tracking, budget management. A
recruiter unfamiliar with CIM needs the mapping made explicit, so Education carries the module
content rather than just the title.

**4. Physical brand application is foregrounded — because it is an airline.**
*"Comfortable working across a wide range of creative disciplines, from physical environments
and livery to digital, content, and service design."* RTA gives her environmental graphics,
wayfinding, kiosks and a two-day physical brand environment; DAIS gives signage and entry
systems. Very few brand-manager applicants have run brand in physical space at that scale.

---

## ⚠️ The gap to name, not hide

**"Minimum of 5 years' experience in a brand and marketing role within a global, matrixed
organisation."**

The years are not the problem — 22 against 5. **The matrixed organisation is.** She has
*served* ministries, government authorities and a media group; she has not *worked inside* a
global matrixed corporate. Her employers have been an agency, her own studio, a newspaper and
independent contracts.

**The honest analogue, which the letter uses:** brand architecture across group subsidiary
businesses in four countries is the closest thing she has to multi-entity brand governance, and
holding creative standards across a ministry programme with a large multidisciplinary team is
the closest thing to navigating an organisation that does not report to her. **Both are real.
Neither is the same as being an employee of a matrixed multinational, and the letter does not
pretend otherwise.**

**Also not claimed anywhere:** aviation, travel, hospitality or FMCG background (*preferred*,
not required); trademark governance; livery management.

## ⚠️ And the open question that keeps resurfacing

*"Lead evergreen content production, including briefing, shoot planning, permits, on-set
guidance, post-production quality control"* — this is the **third** posting in a week where
directing shoots is a core responsibility.

`application-history.md` has carried an unconfirmed claim since 25 July that the CPI entry
*"directed editorial photo shoots, including concept, styling direction and on-set direction."*
**It is not in this CV**, because it is not in the master. **Confirm it or strike it** — if it
is real it belongs in `cv-source-of-truth.md` and it materially strengthens this application
and every editorial one.
