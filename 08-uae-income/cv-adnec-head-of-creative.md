## The four tailoring decisions

**1. The header claims a discipline, not a current title.** She is titled *Senior Art Director,
Brand and Creative Lead* at WeDo, and a recruiter will verify that. But she has genuinely held
Creative Director roles — Social Dar, the curriculum programme, DAIS. So the header reads
**Creative Leadership**, the summary states plainly that she has *held Creative Director roles*,
and the Experience section gives her real WeDo title accurately. **Nothing overstated, and the
level is established before anyone reaches the current title.**

**2. Multi-cluster brand architecture leads the summary.** Their hardest requirement is *"define
and execute the creative vision across ADNEC Group and its business clusters"* — ADNEC has
seven. Brand architecture across group subsidiaries in four countries is the closest thing any
applicant is likely to bring, and it is buried in the middle of the master CV.

**3. Team leadership is made countable.** They ask for *at least 7 years leading and developing
high-performing creative teams.* Her record carries roughly **ten** — Al Arab 2010–2015,
the Ministry programme 2019–2021, Social Dar 2021–2024, WeDo 2025–present. The CV now makes
that easy to add up rather than leaving a screener to infer it.

**4. AI is promoted into the summary, framed as governance and adoption.** *"Expand the use of
AI-powered content creation tools"* is a stated responsibility. Framed as workflow, quality and
team adoption — not as tooling.

**Client names in full.** UAE Ministry of Education, Dubai Municipality, Roads and Transport
Authority, Dubai Sports Council. In this market recognition is the credential, which is the
exact inverse of every EU variant.
