## 1. The raise conversation — WeDo, September 2026

**This is the highest-value item in the folder.** Per `move-or-stay.md`, a +AED 2,500/month
raise beats the net gain from changing jobs, and +AED 4,000 delivers half the entire savings
target — with zero confidentiality risk, zero probation, zero reference damage, and it raises
the basic salary her gratuity is calculated on.

**Timing: September or early October 2026**, before annual budgets close. Not December.

**Frame: the title does not describe the job.** Ask about title and salary together. It reads
as commitment and ambition — the opposite of a leaving signal — which is exactly what she
needs it to read as.

### The five things to put on the table

1. **Title versus remit.** She is titled *Senior Art Director* while leading creative vision for
   government and institutional clients, owning senior client relationships, and presenting and
   defending direction to ministers. That is a Creative Director remit.
2. **The commercial number.** A **12 percent government tender win rate** contribution, in a
   business that lives on tenders. This is the argument a managing director understands.
3. **The flagship.** **Benet7awel for Dubai Municipality** — a phased change campaign carrying
   an entire headquarters through a shift in working culture.
4. **The scarce capability.** She leads AI adoption in creative production and has written AI
   usage guidelines into client brand systems. **AI capability is a named salary premium in the
   UAE market in 2026** ([Arabian Business](https://www.arabianbusiness.com/jobs/uae-salary-guide-2026-the-fastest-growing-sectors-top-paying-jobs-and-skills-employers-want-most)).
5. **The market.** UAE salaries are forecast up **4.1 percent in 2026**. A flat salary is a
   real-terms cut, and that is a neutral fact rather than a complaint.

### What not to say

- **No mention of other offers, real or implied.** She has none, it is checkable, and a bluff
  that fails ends the relationship she needs for the next ten months.
- **No mention of external interest, recruiters, or "what I could get elsewhere."** Every one of
  those is a leaving signal to a boss who already monitors her LinkedIn.
- **No ultimatum.** She is not willing to leave over this and should not pretend otherwise.

### Numbers — revised 31 Jul 2026

Sara's stated requirement is **+AED 10,000/month, to a floor of AED 23,000–25,000**. Be clear
about what this conversation can and cannot deliver: **AED 14,000 → AED 24,000 is roughly +70%,
and no raise conversation produces that.** UAE salaries move 4.1% in 2026; a strong
promotion-plus-correction runs 15–30%.

**So ask high and expect a correction, not a transformation.**

- **Open at the title first**, then the number. *"The role I'm doing is a Creative Director
  role — I'd like the title to reflect it, and the salary to reflect the market for it."*
- **Anchor: AED 23,000**, citing the market rather than her needs. She is currently **below the
  Cooper Fitch all-roles Dubai median of AED 20,000** and below ERI's model for an
  *eight-year* agency Creative Director — at 22 years. That is the argument, and it is factual.
- **Realistic outcome: +AED 2,000–4,000 plus the title.** Take it. It is free money, it raises
  the basic her gratuity is calculated on, and the title is worth real money at the next
  employer and on every CV she sends.
- **The gap is not closed here, and that is expected.** `market-rate.md` finds AED 23–25k is a
  stretch against agency data and unremarkable against corporate data — so the rest of the gap
  comes from an employer-type change, not from this conversation.

If the answer is no on salary, the fallback asks
in order of value: the **title change** (worth real money at the next employer and on every CV
she sends), a **fixed bonus tied to tender wins**, or **CIM fees covered** — though see the
clawback warning below before accepting that one.

> ⚠️ **Check the contract before asking WeDo to fund anything.** `move-or-stay.md`: documented
> training costs are recoverable proportionately on resignation. **If WeDo already funds CIM
> Level 7, that is a live repayment exposure on her eventual departure and she needs to know the
> number now.** Do not add new employer-funded training in 2026 without reading the clause.

## 3. The confidentiality briefing — send before any CV, every time

**This is non-optional and it goes first, in writing.** UAE recruiters routinely submit CVs to
clients without asking, and WeDo's clients and competitors overlap heavily with any target list.

> Before we go further, one condition on my side: my search is strictly confidential and my
> current employer must not learn of it. Please do not submit my CV, or name me, to any company
> without my written approval for that specific company first.
>
> Two related asks: please tell me which of your clients I should be excluded from, and please
> don't name my current clients in any summary you write — a federal transport authority and a
> municipal government will do.
>
> If that works, I'll send my CV across today.

**A recruiter who ignores this has told her something useful, early, at no cost.**

## 3b. The warm message — an existing 1st-degree connection

**The best channel available anywhere in this folder**, and it exists for ITP: Natasha
Pendleton, Group Publishing Director, is already a 1st-degree connection. A DM to an existing
connection is completely private — no application, no follow, no observable action.

**The move: use a below-level posting as the reason to ask about the level above it.** ITP runs
100+ brands; the Art Director post on T MENA is one door, and the Group Art Director and
Creative Director roles are the ones that match her.

> Hi Natasha,
>

> English–Arabic editorial is unusually close to my own background — I've built typographic

>
> I'm at senior creative leadership level now — currently leading brand and creative for
> government and institutional clients, with a team — so the Art Director post itself sits a
> step below where I am. But it made me want to ask you directly: is ITP building at Group Art
> Director or Creative Director level across any of the titles?
>
> Happy to send my portfolio either way. And I'd be grateful to keep this between us — my
> current employer isn't aware I'm looking.

**Why it is worded that way:** it names the level honestly rather than applying downward and
hoping, it gives a reason for the approach that is about the work rather than about wanting
out, it asks one specific answerable question, and **the confidentiality line comes from her
rather than being assumed** — with a 1st-degree connection in a small market, that sentence is
the whole protection.

## What decides whether any UAE application actually gets sent

The five conditions in `move-or-stay.md`, all of which must hold:

1. Uplift **≥ AED 6,000/month**
2. **Tier-1 employer** with a real European end
3. **Start date on or before 1 November 2026**
4. Contract clean — probation ≤ 3 months, no training clawback, notice ≤ 60 days, non-compete
   limited
5. **No break in UAE residency** — a transfer, not a cancel-and-reissue

**On today's date, condition 3 means an offer would need to be agreed within roughly eight
weeks.** In practice that happens only if a tier-1 employer already has something open at her
level — which is precisely what the four direct approaches are for, and why they are worth
sending even though the base case is stay and negotiate.

**Everything sent gets logged in `05-career-context/application-history.md`.**
