# UAE income — the funding engine

Owned by the **UAE jobs session**.

## Why this exists

`01-relocation/pipeline-jobs.md` records a hard constraint:

> **No capital to self-fund.** Any route requiring a blocked account, a savings deposit or
> months of living costs with no income is **out**. This removes every "come and look for
> work" permit in the EU.

That single line closes more doors than her passport does. It rules out Germany's
Opportunity Card, the Dutch orientation year, and every EU route that asks for proof of
funds, leaving only two channels: an employer who sponsors her, or a remote salary
converting to a digital nomad visa.

**Savings reopen those doors.** So a higher UAE salary is not a side quest, it is
infrastructure for the whole plan. This folder treats UAE earning as a funding engine with
a target number and a deadline, not as a vague ambition to be paid more.

## The two things that make a UAE move worth more than its salary

**1. An employer with European offices.** A Creative Director job at a multinational with
Amsterdam, Dublin, London or Brussels offices carries an internal-transfer route that a
local agency job does not, at the same or better pay. An intra-company transfer is often
the single easiest way for a non-EU national to reach Europe, because the employer already
knows her and the paperwork is routine for them.

**Weight this heavily. A slightly lower offer from a company with EU offices can be worth
more than the highest bidder locally.**

**2. Contract terms that do not trap her.** She intends to leave by mid-2027. A new role
signed in late 2026 gives her months, not years. Notice periods, probation, relocation
clawbacks and any training-cost repayment clause all matter more than usual.

## The two findings that were not expected

**1. Cutting spending is worth exactly as much as a raise, and more than a job move.** UAE
income is untaxed, so a dirham saved equals a dirham earned — a AED 1,000/month housing cut is
identical to a AED 1,000 raise, with no career risk. In the Netherlands the same €1 of saving
requires ~€1.85 of gross salary. **Every tax-free month here is worth 1.6–1.8 months of
European saving capacity at the same seniority, and there are ten left.**

**2. Freelance income is the only option that does two jobs at once.** It funds the target
faster than a raise or a job move — and Spain's digital nomad visa **explicitly accepts
freelancers**, needs clients outside Spain, and requires three months of the relationship.
Freelance started in September 2026 is nine months of provable recurring non-Spanish income by
May 2027. **Nothing else both funds the move and builds a route.** Subject to the exclusivity
and freelance-permit checks in `move-or-stay.md`.

## ⛔ The one email that is worth €19,000

Spain's DNV financial test can be met by **income** (€2,849/month) **or** by **savings of
€34,188**. The vault has always assumed the income limb — correctly in principle, and it is why
Route A2 survived the no-capital constraint. **But if the Spanish mission in Abu Dhabi requires
a bank balance alongside the contract, Route A2 needs €34,188, not €12,800, and it closes on
this timeline.**

**One email to the embassy in August 2026 answers it.** Highest-value unresolved question in the
plan, and it costs nothing. Full detail in `savings-target.md`.
