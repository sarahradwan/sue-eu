# The target number

Owned by the **UAE jobs session**. Set 31 July 2026.

This is step 1 and everything else in this folder is subordinate to it. Without a figure and
a date, "earn more" has no stopping condition and no test for whether a job move was worth it.

---

## THE ANSWER

> ## €15,000 — AED 63,750 — liquid and in her own account by **31 May 2027**.
>
> From August 2026 that is **€1,500/month saved (AED 6,375)**, plus about **€135/month
> (AED 575)** of preparation spend on top.
>
> **Total cash to find: ~AED 6,950 every month for ten months.**

At a mid-range Abu Dhabi lifestyle that implies a total package of roughly
**AED 18,000–20,000/month**. The full working is below.

**Exchange rate used throughout: AED 4.25 = €1.** The dirham is pegged to the dollar at
3.6725, so the only moving part is EUR/USD. Re-check before relying on a conversion; a swing
to AED 4.00 makes the target AED 60,000, a swing to AED 4.50 makes it AED 67,500.

## Why 31 May 2027 and not "before I leave"

Three separate facts push the deadline forward of the departure date, and all three are
already recorded in the vault.

1. **Permits are decided in spring 2027** (`timeline-to-2027.md`). Deposits, flights,
   insurance and translations are paid *before* the first European salary, not after.
2. **The end-of-service gratuity cannot be part of this number.** It pays on termination —
   so it arrives *after* she resigns, around June–July 2027. `pipeline-scholarships.md`
   already reached this conclusion: **treat the gratuity as arrival money, not permit
   money.** The €15,000 has to come from salary.
3. **Money that arrives in August 2027 buys nothing.** By then the decisions are made. May
   is the last month where cash still converts into options.

## ⛔ THE ONE UNKNOWN THAT COULD MOVE THIS NUMBER BY €19,000

Spain's digital nomad visa financial test can be satisfied **either** by income — contract
plus payslips at €2,849/month — **or** by savings equal to the annual figure, **€34,188**
([Jobbatical](https://www.jobbatical.com/blog/spain-digital-nomad-visa-income-smi-increase),
[Anchorless](https://anchorless.io/blog/sp-moving/spain-digital-nomad-visa)).

The vault has always assumed the income limb, which is correct in principle and is why A2
survived the no-capital constraint at all. **But consulates vary, and several ask for bank
statements alongside the contract.** If the Spanish mission in Abu Dhabi wants savings rather
than accepting income alone, Route A2's requirement is not €12,800 — it is **€34,188**, and
the route is closed on this timeline.

**Action: email the Spanish Embassy in Abu Dhabi in August 2026 and ask one question** —
*does the digital nomad visa application accept proof of monthly income alone, or is a
minimum bank balance also required?* They publish a dedicated DNV page, so there is a
department that answers this.

One email, answered in weeks, decides whether a whole route exists. **It is the
highest-value unresolved question in the plan and it costs nothing.** Resolve it now, not
in spring 2027 when the answer cannot be acted on.

## The lever nobody counts: cutting burn is worth more than a raise

UAE income is untaxed, so **a dirham not spent is worth exactly a dirham earned.** Nowhere
in Europe is that true. In the Netherlands, saving €1 requires roughly €1.85 of gross salary
once income tax is paid.

So cutting AED 3,000/month of spending is worth precisely as much as a AED 3,000/month raise
— and it takes one afternoon rather than one job change, one probation period and one burnt
reference.

**Concretely: moving from a AED 4,500 one-bedroom to a AED 3,500 studio closes AED 1,000 of
the AED 6,950 gap on its own, permanently, with no career risk whatsoever.**

This is also the reason not to rush out of the UAE. **Every tax-free month here is worth
roughly 1.6–1.8 months of European saving capacity at the same seniority.** The UAE months
are the cheapest euros she will ever bank, and there are only ten of them left.
