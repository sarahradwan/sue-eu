# ATS-safe CV template

Master template. Copy, customise per application, log in `application-history.md`.
Never overwrite this file with a tailored version.

**Why this exists:** her designed CV uses two 2-column tables and puts contact details in
the header — both common ATS parse failures. This version is deliberately plain. It is not
a downgrade of her design; it is a different document for a different reader.
See `.claude/skills/application-tailor/SKILL.md`.

## Rules — do not break these when customising

- Single column. No tables, no panels, no columns.
- Contact details in the **body**, never the header or footer.
- Standard headings only: `Experience` `Education` `Skills` `Languages`.
  Not "Across Brand, Experience & Technology" — a parser cannot classify it.
- Standard bullets (`-`), never `▸`.
- Dates as `Month Year – Month Year`, consistently.
- Title, employer, location, dates each parseable.
- Export as **.docx** unless PDF is demanded.
- Two pages is fine at 22 years. Do not compress to one and lose the evidence.

## Customising per application

1. Rewrite the **Summary** for the specific role — this is the highest-value edit.
2. Reorder **Experience bullets** so what they prioritise comes first. Never reorder roles
   out of reverse-chronological order.
3. Adjust **Skills** to mirror their vocabulary — using words she has earned, never
   inventing.
4. Cut what is irrelevant. A shorter targeted CV beats a complete one.
5. Never invent a number, tool, title or date.
