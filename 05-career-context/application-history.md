## Patterns

Reviewed at each `skill-retro`. Rejections carry information — which routes stall, which
framings land, whether the sponsorship question kills applications early or late.

- **⛔ The permit floor is a *title* floor. First real pattern, found 25 Jul 2026 from the
  AKQA Senior Designer rejection.** That application was structurally un-winnable and the
  rejection says nothing about her. Senior Designer in Amsterdam pays roughly **€54,000
  average, €44,251–€67,500** — **the entire band sits below the ~€71,300 highly-skilled-migrant
  threshold.** Even at the 75th percentile AKQA could not have sponsored her on that role.
  Applying below her level does not improve her odds of getting in; it guarantees the visa
  cannot follow.

  Rough Amsterdam bands against the ~€71,300 floor:

  | Level | Reported band | Clears the floor? |
  |---|---|---|
  | Senior Designer | €44k–€68k | ❌ **Never** |
  | Art Director | €50k–€79k | ⚠️ **Only at the top** — marginal, verify per posting |
  | Senior Art Director | higher, varies | ✅ Usually |
  | Associate Creative Director / Creative Director | €80k–€122k, avg €98.5k | ✅ **Comfortably** |

  **Rule: do not apply below Senior Art Director / Associate Creative Director.** Not pride —
  arithmetic. Sources disagree on Art Director (Glassdoor reports €50k–€79k, SalaryExpert
  models ~€99k+); reported data is the safer guide for what an offer will actually say.

- **Open question carried from the first tailoring pass (25 Jul 2026):** the CPI entry now
