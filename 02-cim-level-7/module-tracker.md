## The distinction that matters for planning

**Global Marketing Decisions is assessed by exam. The other two are assignments.**

These need opposite preparation shapes and cannot be planned the same way:

- **Exam** — fixed sitting date, no extensions, revision must be front-loaded, and CIM
  exams run in set windows. If a resit is needed it can be months away, which is the risk
  worth planning around given the 2027 timeline.
- **Assignments** — fixed submission deadline, work is continuous, and the organisation
  chosen shapes the whole piece. Front-load the organisational analysis and it carries
  across both.

## Strategic angle — worth being deliberate about

Two of the three modules map directly onto work already underway:

**Global Marketing Decisions** is about assessing strategy for a diverse global
marketplace. Sara is trying to enter the European market. Using European market context
in this module means her required coursework doubles as EU market research. Highest-value
overlap in the whole qualification — it should be used deliberately, not by accident.

**Corporate Digital Communications** covers corporate brand, digital communications,
positioning and brand equity. That is the same problem as her portfolio site and LinkedIn
positioning (`04-portfolio-positioning/`). The module gives her a framework for work she
has to do anyway.

**Creating Entrepreneurial Change** covers innovation, disruption and change. Her AI
adoption at WeDo — embedding generation into live production and codifying AI usage into
client brand documentation — is an unusually strong real case for this. It is genuinely
disruptive practice she personally led, which is exactly what the module asks for.

## Interaction with the EU timeline

Scholarship applications for September 2027 intake open **Oct–Nov 2026**, deadlines
**Nov 2026 – Jan 2027** (`../01-relocation/timeline-to-2027.md`).

Whether the diploma completes before or after that window changes how it is presented:

- **Completes before** — a finished Level 7 postgraduate qualification on the application.
  Materially stronger, and directly relevant for a candidate whose degree is from 2003;
  it demonstrates recent postgraduate-level study, which some schemes require.
- **Completes after** — state it as in progress with the expected completion date. Normal
  and accepted; institutions handle this routinely.

Either way it strengthens the application. **The one thing to avoid is a clash** — an exam
sitting or assignment deadline landing in the same weeks as scholarship deadlines. That is
the specific thing to check once dates are known, and the reason the dates matter now
rather than later.
