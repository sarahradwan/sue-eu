# Egypt and remote USD income

Opened 25 October 2026 at Sara's request: Egyptian opportunities at EGP 80,000 and
preferably EGP 100,000 net or more, plus remote roles paying USD that she can do from Egypt
or anywhere.

**Planning rate: EGP 50 to the US dollar.** Sources put mid-2026 between roughly 49 and 51
and none carried an October figure, so this is a planning assumption, not a quote.
Reconfirm before any salary conversation.

## ⚠️ The finding that reshapes the Egypt half

Her target is **well above the published Egyptian market for her role.**

| Benchmark, Payscale Egypt | Annual EGP | Monthly EGP |
|---|---|---|
| Creative Director, average | ~178,000 | ~14,800 |
| Creative Director, 90th percentile | ~429,000 | ~35,750 |
| Senior Art Director, average | ~144,000 | ~12,000 |
| Senior Art Director, 75th percentile | ~301,000 | ~25,000 |

Sources: [Payscale Creative Director Egypt](https://www.payscale.com/research/EG/Job=Creative_Director/Salary) ·
[Payscale Sr. Art Director Egypt](https://www.payscale.com/research/EG/Job=Sr._Art_Director/Salary)

**EGP 100,000 a month is EGP 1,200,000 a year. That is roughly 2.8 times the 90th percentile
on that data.**

Payscale skews low, is self-reported, and mixes junior titles into national averages, so
treat the absolute figures as soft. **The gap is far too large to be measurement noise.**

**What that means in practice:** EGP 80,000 to 100,000 net exists in Egypt, but not in the
ordinary agency market. It sits in a narrow band:

1. **Multinational Egypt offices.** P&G, Unilever, PepsiCo, Mondelez, Vodafone, Orange and
   similar pay well above local agencies for senior in-house brand roles.
2. **Offshore and delivery centres** serving Gulf or European clients, often with
   USD-linked pay.
3. **Gulf-owned businesses with Cairo operations**, paying closer to regional than local
   rates.
4. **Anything denominated in USD rather than EGP.**

Those four are the search. A Cairo creative agency almost certainly is not.

## The currency risk nobody should ignore

A salary fixed in Egyptian pounds loses purchasing power as the pound moves. **An
EGP-denominated EGP 100,000 today is not EGP 100,000 in real terms in two years.**

So on the Egypt track, **a USD-denominated or USD-linked contract is worth materially more
than a higher EGP number.** This should be a screening criterion, not an afterthought.

## ✅ The remote USD track is the strongest thing in the whole plan

This is the half worth prioritising, and the reason is arithmetic.

| | Monthly | In EGP at 50 |
|---|---|---|
| Her stated Egypt floor | EGP 80,000 | 80,000 |
| Her stated Egypt preference | EGP 100,000 net | 100,000 |
| **Spain's digital nomad income test (€2,849)** | **~USD 3,080** | **~154,000** |
| Her current UAE package (AED 13–15k) | ~USD 3,540–4,085 | ~177,000–204,000 |

**A remote role at USD 3,100 a month pays about 1.5 times her stated Egypt preference, and
it satisfies the Spanish income limb on its own.**

That is not a modest salary for a senior creative in the US or UK market. It is ordinary.

**Why this one route does four things at once:**

| It solves | How |
|---|---|
| **Income** | Above her Egypt target, in hard currency, with no pound devaluation risk |
| **The Spanish route** | `pipeline-jobs.md` Route A2 needs a non-Spanish employer, three months of relationship, and €2,849/month. This is exactly that shape |
| **The employer problem** | `CLAUDE.md` records that her current employer will not assist with anything. A new remote employer *is* the assistance |
| **Location freedom** | Works from Egypt, the UAE, or anywhere. It does not trap her in one market |

**One caution carried over from `roadmap-v2-review.md`:** a foreign *employer* may need to
register with Spanish Social Security, since there is no Egypt or UAE coordination agreement
with Spain. **Freelance and self-employed applicants handle their own contributions**, which
is why self-employed A2 may be cleaner than employed A2. Verify before relying on either.

## 🔓 What going home actually unlocks, and it is not money

The underrated advantage of Egypt has nothing to do with salary.

**She is an Egyptian citizen. In Egypt, no employer owns her residency.**

Right now her UAE residence visa is tied to WeDo. That is the root of several constraints in
this repo: the confidentiality rule, the NOC question for freelance work, the risk that
being discovered costs her both the job and the right to stay.

**In Egypt all of that disappears.** She can freelance legally without anyone's permission,
build USD income openly, and apply for a Spanish permit from a position where no employer
can retaliate.

**Egypt is not a step toward Europe. It is a step out of dependency.** That is worth naming
properly, because it is the real argument for the track, and it is stronger than the salary
argument.

## The honest cost

Moving to Egypt on EGP 100,000 net is roughly **half her current income in dollar terms.**
Cairo costs materially less than Abu Dhabi, so it may net out close to flat, but it is a cut
in absolute earning and therefore in saving capacity toward any move.

**That trade is only clearly good if the income is USD-denominated.** In pounds it is a
lateral move with currency risk attached. In dollars it is a straightforward improvement on
every axis.

## What the session should do

1. **Lead with the remote USD search, not the Egypt search.** Better pay, portable, and it
   opens a route. Egypt-based roles are the fallback.
2. **Screen Egypt roles for USD denomination first**, seniority second.
3. **Target the four employer categories above.** Do not spend time on Cairo agency
   postings; the published market does not reach her number.
4. **Treat USD 3,100/month as the threshold that matters**, since it clears the Spanish
   income test. Anything at or above it is strategically different from anything below.
5. **Verify the EGP rate** before any offer conversation.
