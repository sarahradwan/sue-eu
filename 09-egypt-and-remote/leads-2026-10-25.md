oversees design, visual and multimedia production, with one or two reports. That sits

Annual Report as proof she runs a bilingual publication end to end, not just art directs
it. They also say they may hire an exceptional candidate one level up, as Communications
Director.

firm that is remote and open to Egypt or the UAE. Radley Yeldar and Black Sun posted
London office roles only, at junior to mid level.
