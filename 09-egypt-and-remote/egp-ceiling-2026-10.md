# What a local EGP payer can realistically offer

25 October 2026. Answers one question: what is the best salary from an Egyptian company
paying in pounds?

## The answer

**About EGP 60,000 to 90,000 gross a month for a creative-titled role. Roughly EGP 45,000
to 70,000 net.** This is an estimate, not a quote. Reasoning below.

EGP 80,000 net is the edge of possible. EGP 100,000 net from a local EGP payer almost
never comes with a creative title. It needs about EGP 135,000 gross, which these surveys
put at General Manager level.

## How the estimate was built

Local companies pay marketing leadership, not creative. Creative Director tops out in the
30,000s. Head of Marketing and Marketing Director reach 100,000 and above.

So the ceiling assumes a director-level title, Head of Brand or Brand and Communications
Director, at the top local payers: large banks (CIB, Banque Misr), large developers (TMG,
Palm Hills), large tech (Fawry).

Net assumes the 27.5% top income tax rate plus capped social insurance, leaving about
75 to 80% of gross at this level. Check with an Egyptian accountant before relying on it.

## Why this is the weakest option in the plan

EGP 100,000 is about USD 2,000 at the planning rate of 50, which still needs
reconfirming. That is under the USD 3,100 line, about half her UAE pay, and it loses value
as the pound moves. Fallback only.

**If she pursues it:** search Head of Brand and Marketing Director titles at the employers
above. Skip Creative Director and Art Director postings.
