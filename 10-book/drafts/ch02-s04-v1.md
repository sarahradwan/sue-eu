# Chapter Two, section 4. Draft v1, 2 November 2026

From her answers to Q5, Q6 and the correction about who knew. About 230 words. Uses wording
option 1 (management never knew), which she has not yet confirmed.

---

They flew me from Cairo to be the artistic side of the department. I had a special desk, and
the latest Apple desktop, a machine worth fourteen thousand Qatari riyals in 2013.

Most of the Doha team rejected me. The art director too. Because I was a woman. And not only a
woman. A successful one.

One day I was in the corridor, by chance, and I heard one of them inside the design room.

"I'll go back to my home country and beg in the streets if she becomes my manager."

He didn't even know me.

I did nothing. Honestly, I laughed.

A few months later, I understood why he was so worried. He and his best friend had installed a
game on the work computer. Hide and seek, with guns. They played it almost all day, and took
breaks from the game to work.

Management never knew. I was never a snitch, and I had no official authority to ban it. I wish
I could have. It could have been a break-time thing, not an everyday routine. Sometimes it
delayed the work.

They were scared of me being powerful. That was my power over them.
