## What this changes

- **The book is not the Arabic and multi-script practitioner book** that the README and
  the ghostwriter skill assumed. It is personal: a life told in essays, with self-mocking
  chapter titles. The design career is part of the life, not the subject.
- The chapter titles point to a **thematic** shape: one trait or tension per chapter. The
  introduction carries the chronology (Egypt, Qatar, Dubai).
