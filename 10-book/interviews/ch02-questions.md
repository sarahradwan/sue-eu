# Chapter Two questions, asked one at a time

Built from both Monday question lists, de-duplicated, and kept to things not covered in the
intro or Chapter One. Asked in this order. Answers logged under each, close to verbatim.
Order can change if an answer opens something better.

## A. Recognition in Qatar
1. What had you designed that made the editor-in-chief call you?
2. How did it reach you? Desk, phone, someone passing a message? Had you met them before?
3. What did they say, roughly, and in Arabic or English?
4. What did you do in the hour after?

## B. One incident from the "male circus"
5. Pick one: the "second wife" offer, a rumour, or something else. Who, where, what was said?
6. What did you do in the moment, and what did you do that evening?
7. Did it change how you worked afterwards? One concrete thing.

## C. Your own sabotage
8. One decision where you damaged your own career, not someone else's doing. What was it?
9. A professional disaster that is funny now.

## D. The business
10. How did it start? First client, first invoice, where you worked from.
11. The co-founder: how do they appear in this story, if at all?
12. The day it felt real. A contract, a number, a purchase, an ordinary Tuesday?
13. What did success do to you? Honest answer.
14. The first warning sign you now recognise.
15. The moment you knew it was over. Where were you?
16. What surprised you about yourself afterwards?

## E. Looking back
17. One "failure" you are now glad happened, and one "success" that mattered less than you thought.

**Facts established**

  "grid-perfect boxed designs".
- Hired as team leader. Cairo design department started as her plus one designer. Grew by her
  training people and by hiring skilled designers. Cairo became almost half the design team.
- The Kahramaa design led the sales director to call her. [CHECK: what the Kahramaa piece was]
- Al Arab invited her to Qatar: five-star hotel, pocket money, "to see the country and get me
  used to it", to attract her to move. About one month. The offer to move to Doha came after
  she returned.

**Her phrases to keep**
- "my style hit them hard"
- "they were used to grid-perfect boxed designs"
- "the interaction of picture with text"

**What this changes**
- The intro presents the move as escape ("I just needed air"). Her answer shows she was also
  courted: they flew her out and put her up to win her over. Both can be true, and together
  they are the better story. The intro line "So I accepted the promotion" undersells it.
- The editor-in-chief calls and the sales director call are separate. When and how the
  editor-in-chief called is still unanswered.
- "Découpe" needs one plain sentence of explanation for a general reader.
