# Chapter Two questions, asked one at a time

Built from both Monday question lists, de-duplicated, and kept to things not covered in the
intro or Chapter One. Asked in this order. Answers logged under each, close to verbatim.
Order can change if an answer opens something better.

## A. Recognition in Qatar
1. What had you designed that made the editor-in-chief call you?
2. How did it reach you? Desk, phone, someone passing a message? Had you met them before?
3. What did they say, roughly, and in Arabic or English?
4. What did you do in the hour after?

## A2. Added after Q2

## B. One incident from the "male circus"
5. Pick one: the "second wife" offer, a rumour, or something else. Who, where, what was said?
6. What did you do in the moment, and what did you do that evening?
7. Did it change how you worked afterwards? One concrete thing.

## C. Your own sabotage
8. One decision where you damaged your own career, not someone else's doing. What was it?
9. A professional disaster that is funny now.

## D. The business
10. How did it start? First client, first invoice, where you worked from.
11. The co-founder: how do they appear in this story, if at all?

13. What did success do to you? Honest answer.
