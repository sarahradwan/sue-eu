# LinkedIn profile optimisation, for EU recruiter search

Written July 2026. Governed by `stealth-positioning` and by the measured data in
`linkedin-content-system.md`, which outranks anything here on voice, cadence and format.

**The premise:** her posting engine works. The profile it points at has not been optimised.
Most EU recruiters will find her through LinkedIn Recruiter keyword search, which runs on
headline, About, Experience text and Skills. Not on posts.

## Audit basis, and its limit

Audited against `05-career-context/cv-source-of-truth.md`, which carries her identity line
and role text. **The live profile was not read.** LinkedIn is not fetchable from this
environment. If the live headline or About differs from the CV identity line, the audit
needs re-running against the real text.

## Risk order of profile edits

This is the part that matters, because it decides sequencing. Ranked by how likely a
colleague is to notice:

| Edit | Recruiter search value | Employer visibility | Do it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Skills** | Highest. Recruiters filter on skills directly | Lowest. Skill additions do not push a notification the way a headline or job change does | **First** |
| **Experience text on past roles** | High. Fully indexed by Recruiter search | Very low. Nobody is notified when a 2019 role gets better bullets | **Second** |
| **About** | High | Low to moderate. No loud notification, but a colleague who visits will read it | **Third** |
| **Headline** | High | **Highest of the four.** Headline changes surface | **Fourth, and smallest** |
| **Current role description** | Moderate | Moderate to high. It is the block a colleague looks at | **Last, or not at all in pass one** |

Do these across four to six weeks, not in one sitting. A profile that changes in five
places on one evening is a pattern. Five changes over a month is maintenance.

### 3. Headline

220 character limit. Three options, weakest employer signal first.

**A. Minimal swap. Lowest risk. Recommended for pass one.**
> Creative Director · Brand, Experience & Design Systems · Multilingual and Multi-Script Identity · AI Governance in Brand Practice

Keeps her existing structure and rhythm, so it reads as a tidy-up rather than a rewrite.
Swaps in the two terms that do the most search work.

**B. Stronger for EU search. Slightly more visible as a change.**
> Creative Director · Design systems and brand architecture for government and institutional clients · Multi-script and multilingual identity · AI governance in brand practice

"Government and institutional clients" is the highest-value phrase available to her and it
is completely safe at home, because it is literally her job and a point of pride in Abu
Dhabi.

**C. If she wants the title ladder corrected too.**
> Creative Director, brand and experience · Design systems, brand governance, multilingual identity · 22 years across Egypt, Qatar and the UAE · CIM Level 7

Worth knowing: in most EU agencies "Art Director" sits *below* Design Director, so a
headline reading "Senior Art Director" is read as mid-weight in Amsterdam even though it is
senior in the Gulf. Headline does not have to match the job title field and commonly does
not.

**Recommendation:** A now, B in six to eight weeks if nothing has been said. Do not do
both in the same month.

## What was deliberately not suggested

Named so she knows the tactic exists and why it is off the table:

| Tactic | Why it raises reach | Why it is unsafe here | Safe equivalent |
|---|---|---|---|
| `#OpenToWork`, open badge | Puts her in the recruiter open-candidate filter | The badge is visible and the recruiter-only version leaks | Skills and Experience keywords, which the same recruiters search |
| Amsterdam or Brussels in the location field | Puts her in geo-filtered searches | The single loudest tell available | Let the *work* read as international. Location stays Abu Dhabi |
| "Open to Creative Director roles in Europe" in About | Converts recruiter visits at a much higher rate | Ends the job | An About that reads as international director level with no stated intent |
| A `+31` number on the profile | Removes friction for EU recruiters calling | Specific, and has only one explanation | Dutch number on direct applications and CVs only, never public |
| Following 40 EU recruiters | Triggers reciprocal profile views | Following patterns are visible and reciprocal-follow is a known job-hunt shape | Comment on studios and institutions instead. See `eu-engagement-list.md` |
| Joining "Design jobs in Netherlands" groups | Direct exposure to hiring posts | Group membership is on the public profile | Follow the ECL, EU institutions and studios as professional interest |
| Adding German A1 to the profile | Small keyword value | One of the very few genuine tells available to a watcher | Leave it off until it is worth listing on its own merit |
