# LinkedIn strategy

> **Her employer monitors this profile.** Every rule below follows from that.
> Full rules: `.claude/skills/stealth-positioning/SKILL.md`.

## The objective

Findable and credible to EU recruiters. Reads to her boss as ordinary professional pride
in her current role. Both, or it has failed.

| Phone number | **UAE number only** | See below — a Dutch number here is a direct tell |

## The Dutch number

A Netherlands mobile number is genuinely useful for applications — EU recruiters call, and
a local number removes friction and reads as commitment. But it is **contact detail, not
public profile**.

- **Never** on LinkedIn. A `+31` number on a monitored profile is arguably a louder signal
  than changing the location field, because it is specific and has only one explanation.
- **Never** on the public portfolio site for the same reason.
- **Yes** on direct applications, CVs sent to named employers, and scholarship forms.
- Keep the UAE number as the public one throughout.

**One caution:** a Dutch number makes her reachable, it does not make her resident. Never
let it imply she already has Dutch residency or the right to work — that unravels at offer
stage and damages trust exactly when it matters most. Reachability is the goal;
`application-tailor` covers stating her actual status straightforwardly.

## What to lead with

**1. AI governance in brand systems.** Her sharpest differentiator, in demand in the EU
right now, and completely safe — at home it reads as thought leadership in her current
role, because it is. Highest return of anything on this list.

**2. Scale and complexity of real work.** €500k national curriculum programme, 100+ books,
5 subjects, 3 scripts. Brand architecture across four countries. Minister-level
stakeholders. Stating what she actually did is not a signal of intent to leave — it is
just an accurate profile. Zero risk, high recruiter value.

**3. CIM Level 7.** UK-accredited, European-recognised, unambiguously reads as
development rather than exit. Safe to post about freely.

**4. European craft vocabulary.** Design systems, brand architecture, experience design,
multi-script typography, brand governance. She does this work already; naming it the way
EU recruiters search makes her findable with no geographic signal.

## Posting

Roughly monthly. Enough to stay visible in feeds, not so much that a change in pattern
becomes conspicuous — a sudden burst of activity is itself a tell.

**Safe subjects:** AI usage guidelines in brand work · design systems craft ·
multi-script typography · CIM study reflections · industry commentary · team and
mentoring · the discipline of institutional brand work.

**Never:** relocation, elsewhere, dissatisfaction, visas, "what's next", job posts,
EU job groups, mass-following EU recruiters.

## German

Keep it **off** the public profile until it is at a level worth listing on its own merit.
At A1 there is no professional upside, and it is one of the few genuine relocation tells
available to someone watching.

## Before publishing anything

The two-reader test, every time:

1. **As her boss** — any hint she is looking? → rewrite.
2. **As an EU recruiter** — international director level, in searchable vocabulary?
   → if not, it is safe but useless. Rewrite.

Both must pass. State that both were checked.
