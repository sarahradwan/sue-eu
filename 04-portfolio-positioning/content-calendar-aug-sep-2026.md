## The three findings that shaped this

### 1. August and September are opposites, and the naive plan gets it backwards

The instinct is to spread the strong EU-facing posts evenly. That is wrong here.

**August: European reach is structurally low.** Belgium, France, Austria, Spain and Portugal
observe 15 August, and northern European agencies run thin through the middle of the month.
Her own calendar, by contrast, is comparatively free.

**September: European agencies are back**, budgets and Q4 planning restart, and hiring picks
up. It is the best month in this window to be visible to them. **And it is the worst month
she has.** Per `02-cim-level-7/module-tracker.md`, September carries the IELTS sitting, the
document attestation chain, and possibly a CIM exam, with IELTS and attestation both
outranking everything else.

So the rule for this window is: **August is for making. September is for publishing.**
Every September post should be drafted, shot and banked before 31 August. If September
arrives and the content is not already written, it will not get written.

### 2. Two dead slots, both avoidable

| Date | What happens | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **Mon 24 Aug** | **UAE public holiday.** Prophet's Birthday falls Tue 25 Aug and the UAE has it gazetted as Monday 24 Aug under the transferable-holiday rule. Subject to moon sighting, so treat as expected rather than confirmed | Her MENA base is offline, and per `linkedin-distribution-mechanics.md` that base is what supplies the early engagement a post needs to get distributed at all. A serious post here starts cold |
| **Mon 31 Aug** | UK summer bank holiday, last Monday in August. Derived from the rule rather than checked against a source | Matters for the remote-employer route, not for the EU one |

Also worth knowing, lower impact: **Mon 3 Aug is the August bank holiday in Ireland.** Her
Aug 3 post is aimed at her employer and her MENA audience, so it costs almost nothing.
**The Netherlands has no August public holidays at all**, which makes it the most reachable
of her three target markets all month.

### 3. The ratio to watch is not the one it looks like

The obvious worry about loading in institutional design content is that her feed starts
reading as a portfolio pitch. That worry is misplaced, and getting it right unlocks the
whole plan.

**Institutional design content is not a signal.** A creative director at an Abu Dhabi agency
posting about design systems, Arabic typography and government brand work is posting about
her job. Her boss would find it more on-brand than a birthday post, not less.

**European subject matter is the thing to ration.** Tour & Taxis, Brussels, EU institutions.
That is what accumulates into a pattern if it repeats.

In this whole nine-week plan there is **exactly one European-subject post**, Tour & Taxis on
10 August. Everything else is her own MENA work, described in vocabulary that happens to be
what EU recruiters search on. That is the dual-audience strategy working as designed, and it
means the institutional weighting can go up faster than a raw count would suggest.

The European reach comes from commenting, which lives on other people's posts and off her
own feed entirely. See `eu-engagement-list.md`.

audience but costs her attention during the IELTS crunch. **Recommendation: leave it.** It is
already conceived, the drafting cost is low, and September attention is the scarcer resource.

**Thu 3 Sep, the AI page in the brand book.** Both `linkedin-strategy.md` and

agencies are actively trying to build. First week Europe is back is the right place for it.

**Mon 14 Sep, the article.** The reason to write one is in `linkedin-content-system.md`:
articles are indexed by Google, feed posts largely are not, so this becomes a permanent
searchable page against her name. The title does most of the work.

Recommended title: **"Design systems at national scale: 100 books, five subjects, three
scripts."** It carries the search vocabulary, the numbers are hers and verified, and there
is no geographic signal anywhere in it. Teaser post Monday, article same day, link in the
first comment.

**Thu 24 Sep, CIM.** Safe to post about freely per both strategy files. It is UK-accredited
and European-recognised and reads unambiguously as development rather than exit. **Frame it
as study reflection, not a milestone announcement**, because her cohort dates are still
unconfirmed in `02-cim-level-7/module-tracker.md` and announcing a milestone she has not hit
is the kind of small inaccuracy that is entirely avoidable.

**The two open slots, 7 and 17 September, are deliberate.** They are hers to fill from
whatever actually happens. Content I invent for them would be fabricated experience, which
the writer skill bans. Her backlog has the "project most proud of" post waiting on a client
to announce publicly, and trendjacking if a real cultural moment lands. If nothing arrives,
a small-object post from ordinary life is her strongest measured format anyway.

## What this changes, in one line each

- **August stops being a performance month and becomes a production month.** Europe is not
  watching anyway.
- **September carries the EU weight**, and is fully banked before it starts, because her own
  September is committed elsewhere.
- **Three carousels enter the mix**, first one when the stakes are low.
- **One article**, chosen for what Google does with it rather than for its reach.
- **Two slots left genuinely open**, because invented personal content is worse than a gap.
- **European subject matter stays at one post in nine weeks.** The European audience is built
  in the comments, not in the feed.
