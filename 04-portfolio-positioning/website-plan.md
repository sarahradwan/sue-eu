# Portfolio website — sarahradwan.me

Public, so `stealth-positioning` applies — but with more room than LinkedIn, because it is
less directly monitored.

## The dual-market problem

One site, two audiences:

- **UAE clients and her current employer** — must read as a working creative director's
  portfolio. Nothing that suggests she is leaving.
- **EU recruiters and hiring directors** — must read as someone operating at international
  director level, with work whose scale is legible to people who have never heard of RTA
  or WeDo.

The good news: these mostly do not conflict. Depth, rigour and clear outcomes serve both.
The one thing that breaks it is any stated intent to relocate. Contact details and
availability are fine; "seeking opportunities in Europe" is not.

## The core problem to solve: translation

Her portfolio is MENA-institutional. The client names carry enormous weight in the region
and near-zero recognition in Rotterdam or Dublin. So **case studies must carry their own
scale** rather than relying on the name.

Weak: *"Brand experience for RTA."*

Strong: *"A two-day brand experience inside a government building for Dubai's roads and
transport authority — concept, identity, six bilingual Arabic/English activations,
touchscreen kiosks, a participatory installation, a secure registration platform handling
250+ vacancies, environmental graphics, and on-site direction across both days."*

Same project. The second one is legible to someone who has never heard of RTA, and it is
simply more accurate.

Apply the same treatment throughout: say what the organisation *is*, then the scope,
then the outcome.

**3 scripts** · **5 subjects** · **+38%** · **4 countries** · **20+ brands** ·
**12% tender win rate** · **22 years**.
